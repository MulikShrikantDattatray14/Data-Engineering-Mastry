# 12. SQL Seller With No Sales

## Question and image

Report the names of all sellers who did not make any sales in 2020. Include sellers with no orders at all and sellers whose orders were only in other years. Return the result ordered by `seller_name` ascending.

**Example 1**

Input — `Customer`:

| customer_id | customer_name |
|---:|---|
| 101 | Alice |
| 102 | Bob |
| 103 | Charlie |

Input — `Orders`:

| order_id | sale_date | order_cost | customer_id | seller_id |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 2020-03-01 | 1500 | 101 | 1 |
| 2 | 2020-05-25 | 2400 | 102 | 2 |
| 3 | 2019-05-25 | 800 | 101 | 3 |
| 4 | 2020-09-13 | 1000 | 103 | 2 |
| 5 | 2019-02-11 | 700 | 101 | 2 |

Input — `Seller`:

| seller_id | seller_name |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Daniel |
| 2 | Elizabeth |
| 3 | Frank |

Output:

| seller_name |
|---|
| Frank |

Frank has a sale in 2019 but none in 2020. Daniel and Elizabeth each have at least one sale in 2020.
