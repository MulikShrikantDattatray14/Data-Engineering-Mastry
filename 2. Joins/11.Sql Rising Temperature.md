# 11. SQL Rising Temperature

## Question and image

The `Weather` table records one temperature for each `recordDate`. Find the IDs of dates whose temperature is higher than on the previous calendar day (yesterday).

**Example 1**

Input — `Weather`:

| id | recordDate | temperature |
|---:|---|---:|
| 1 | 2015-01-01 | 10 |
| 2 | 2015-01-02 | 25 |
| 3 | 2015-01-03 | 20 |
| 4 | 2015-01-04 | 30 |

Output:

| id |
|---:|
| 2 |
| 4 |

January 2 is warmer than January 1 (`25 > 10`), and January 4 is warmer than January 3 (`30 > 20`).
