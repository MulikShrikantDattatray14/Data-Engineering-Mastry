# P031 · Attach the customer names

[![Previous](../previous.svg)](01.md) [![Next](../next.svg)](03.md)  · **2 of 6**

The left join preserves all three orders. Order 10 matches Asha Rao on ID 1. An unmatched customer ID would leave the name fields NULL.

## SQL added or changed

```sql
LEFT JOIN Customers AS c ON o.CustomerID = c.CustomerID
```

## Input to this step

**Start with the orders · 3 rows**

| 🟦 OrderID | 🟦 CustomerID | 🟦 Total_Order_Amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🟦 10 | 🟦 1 | 🟦 300 |
| 🟦 11 | 🟦 2 | 🟦 100 |
| 🟦 12 | 🟦 3 | 🟦 200 |

**🟩 Customers · 3 rows**

| 🟩 CustomerID | 🟩 FirstName | 🟩 LastName |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🟩 1 | 🟩 Asha | 🟩 Rao |
| 🟩 2 | 🟩 Ben | 🟩 Das |
| 🟩 3 | 🟩 Cia | 🟩 Sen |

## Result · 3 rows

| 🟦 OrderID | 🟦 CustomerID | 🟦 Total_Order_Amount | 🟩 matched_customer_id | 🟩 FirstName | 🟩 LastName |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 🟦 10 | 🟦 1 | 🟦 300 | 🟩 1 | 🟩 Asha | 🟩 Rao |
| 🟦 11 | 🟦 2 | 🟦 100 | 🟩 2 | 🟩 Ben | 🟩 Das |
| 🟦 12 | 🟦 3 | 🟦 200 | 🟩 3 | 🟩 Cia | 🟩 Sen |

<details>
<summary>Full SQLite query for this step</summary>

```sql
SELECT o.OrderID, o.CustomerID, o.Total_Order_Amount,
       c.CustomerID AS matched_customer_id, c.FirstName, c.LastName
FROM Orders AS o
LEFT JOIN Customers AS c ON o.CustomerID = c.CustomerID
ORDER BY o.OrderID;
```

</details>
