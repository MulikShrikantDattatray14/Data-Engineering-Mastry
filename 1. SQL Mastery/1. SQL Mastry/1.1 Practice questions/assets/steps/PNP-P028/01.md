# P028 · Why start with Orders?

<kbd>Previous</kbd> [![Next](../next.svg)](02.md)  · **1 of 8**

Orders supplies all five transactions and their amounts. Orders 13 and 14 contribute 50 and 150 even though neither has a usable payment ID.

## SQL added or changed

```sql
SELECT OrderID, PaymentID, Total_Order_Amount FROM Orders ORDER BY OrderID;
```
