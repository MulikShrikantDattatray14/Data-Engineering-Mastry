# P028 · Why group by the displayed payment type?

[![Previous](../previous.svg)](03.md) [![Next](../next.svg)](05.md)  · **4 of 8**

The two UPI orders share one payment group. The Card order forms the other group.

## SQL added or changed

```sql
SELECT COALESCE(p.PaymentType, 'Unknown') AS payment_type, COUNT(*) AS group_rows FROM Orders AS o LEFT JOIN Payments AS p ON o.PaymentID = p.PaymentID GROUP BY payment_type;
```
