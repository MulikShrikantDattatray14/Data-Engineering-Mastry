# P028 · Why name missing payment types before grouping?

[![Previous](../previous.svg)](🟪 02.md) [![Next](../next.svg)](04.md)  · **3 of 8**

Orders 🟦 13 and 🟦 14 both receive 🟩 Unknown. The matched labels on orders 🟦 10–🟦 12 stay 🟩 UPI, 🟩 UPI and 🟩 Card. Each row keeps its original amount.

## SQL added or changed

```sql
SELECT o.OrderID, COALESCE(p.PaymentType, 'Unknown') AS payment_type, o.Total_Order_Amount FROM Orders AS o LEFT JOIN Payments AS p ON o.PaymentID = p.PaymentID ORDER BY o.OrderID;
```
