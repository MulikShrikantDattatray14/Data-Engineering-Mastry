# P028 · Why do the two shares use different denominators?

[![Previous](../previous.svg)](06.md) [![Next](../next.svg)](08.md)  · **7 of 8**

🟩 UPI contributes 🟦 2/🟦 5 = 🟦 40% of orders and 🟦 300/🟦 800 = 🟦 37.50% of value. 🟩 Unknown contributes 🟦 40% and 🟦 25%. 🟩 Card contributes 🟦 20% and 🟦 37.50%.

## SQL added or changed

```sql
SELECT
    COALESCE(Payments.PaymentType, 'Unknown') AS payment_type,
    COUNT(DISTINCT Orders.OrderID) AS orders,
    ROUND(
        COUNT(DISTINCT Orders.OrderID) * 100.0
        / (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM Orders),
        2
    ) AS pct_of_orders,
    SUM(Orders.Total_Order_Amount) AS order_value,
    ROUND(
        SUM(Orders.Total_Order_Amount) * 100.0
        / (SELECT SUM(Total_Order_Amount) FROM Orders),
        2
    ) AS pct_of_value
FROM Orders
LEFT JOIN Payments
    ON Orders.PaymentID = Payments.PaymentID
GROUP BY payment_type;
```
