# P022 · Apply the cutoff and sort

[![Previous](../previous.svg)](01.md) <kbd>Next</kbd>  · **2 of 2**

HAVING checks the group count after aggregation. The order ID gives a consistent result for tied counts.

## SQL added or changed

```sql
having count(OrderID) > 8
order by count(OrderID) desc ,OrderID asc
```

## Input to this step

**Define and check one row per order · 2 rows**

| 🟦 OrderID | 🟦 line_items | 🟦 distinct_products | 🟦 total_units |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10 | 9 | 8 | 9 |
| 11 | 1 | 1 | 1 |

## Result · 1 row

| 🟦 OrderID | 🟦 line_items | 🟦 distinct_products | 🟦 total_units |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10 | 9 | 8 | 9 |

<details>
<summary>Full SQLite query for this step</summary>

```sql
select OrderID,count(OrderID)  as line_items, count(distinct(ProductID)) as distinct_products,
sum(Quantity) as total_units
from OrderDetails
group by OrderID
having count(OrderID) > 8
order by count(OrderID) desc ,OrderID asc
```

</details>
