# PNP P033 · Products that have never been ordered · Why add OrderDetails with a LEFT JOIN?

[![Previous](../previous.svg)](01.md) [![Next](../next.svg)](03.md)  · **2 of 4**

🟩 OrderDetails is added after Products because ProductID is already available from the driving catalogue row. The LEFT JOIN on Products.ProductID = OrderDetails.ProductID preserves every product: Pen matches order-detail row 10, while Cup has no match and receives NULL in the green OrderDetails columns. If one product has several order lines, its matched row repeats once per line at this intermediate stage.

## SQL added or changed

```sql
LEFT JOIN OrderDetails AS OD
    ON P.ProductID = OD.ProductID
```

## Input to this step

**Products · 2 rows**

| 🟦 ProductID | 🟦 Product | 🟦 Brand | 🟦 CategoryID | 🟦 Sale_Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 🟦 1 | 🟦 Pen | 🟦 Nova | 🟦 10 | 🟦 5 |
| 🟦 2 | 🟦 Cup | 🟦 Home | 🟦 20 | 🟦 10 |

**OrderDetails · 1 row**

| 🟩 OrderDetailID | 🟩 OrderID | 🟩 ProductID |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🟩 10 | 🟩 100 | 🟩 1 |

## Result · 2 rows

| 🟦 ProductID | 🟦 Product | 🟦 Brand | 🟦 CategoryID | 🟦 Sale_Price | 🟩 OrderDetailID | 🟩 OrderID | 🟩 matched_ProductID |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 🟦 1 | 🟦 Pen | 🟦 Nova | 🟦 10 | 🟦 5 | 🟩 10 | 🟩 100 | 🟩 1 |
| 🟦 2 | 🟦 Cup | 🟦 Home | 🟦 20 | 🟦 10 | 🟩 NULL | 🟩 NULL | 🟩 NULL |

<details>
<summary>Full SQLite query for this step</summary>

```sql
SELECT P.ProductID, P.Product, P.Brand, P.CategoryID, P.Sale_Price,
       OD.OrderDetailID, OD.OrderID, OD.ProductID AS matched_ProductID
FROM Products AS P
LEFT JOIN OrderDetails AS OD
    ON P.ProductID = OD.ProductID
ORDER BY P.ProductID, OD.OrderDetailID;
```

</details>
