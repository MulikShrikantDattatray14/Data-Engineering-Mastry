# PNP P033 · Products that have never been ordered · Why does Products drive the anti-match check?

<kbd>Previous</kbd> [![Next](../next.svg)](02.md)  · **1 of 4**

Start from 🟦 Products because the requested output is a list of catalogue products, including products that have no order history. Starting from 🟩 OrderDetails would lose exactly those never-ordered products before they could be tested.
