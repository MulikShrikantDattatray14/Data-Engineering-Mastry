# P019 · Group by product and calculate the metrics

[![Previous](../previous.svg)](🟦 01.md) [![Next](../next.svg)](🟦 03.md)  · **🟦 2 of 🟦 3**

The metrics answer different questions. 🟩 Pen's quantities add up to five. Its two lines happen to belong to different orders. If it appears again in order 🟦 10, the line count increases and the distinct order count stays at two.

## SQL added or changed

```sql
select O.ProductID,P.Product,sum (O.Quantity) as units_sold, count(OrderDetailID)  as order_lines, count(distinct(OrderID)) as orders
-- …
left join Products as P on O.ProductID =P.ProductID
group by O.ProductID
ORDER BY O.ProductID;
```

## Input to this step

**Attach the product name to each order line · 🟦 3 rows**

| 🟦 OrderDetailID | 🟦 OrderID | 🟦 ProductID | 🟩 Product | 🟦 Quantity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 🟦 1 | 🟦 10 | 🟦 1 | 🟩 Pen | 🟦 2 |
| 🟦 2 | 🟦 11 | 🟦 1 | 🟩 Pen | 🟦 3 |
| 🟦 3 | 🟦 11 | 🟦 2 | 🟩 Bag | 🟦 1 |

## Result · 🟦 2 rows

| 🟦 ProductID | 🟩 Product | 🟦 units_sold | 🟦 order_lines | 🟦 orders |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 🟦 1 | 🟩 Pen | 🟦 5 | 🟦 2 | 🟦 2 |
| 🟦 2 | 🟩 Bag | 🟦 1 | 🟦 1 | 🟦 1 |
