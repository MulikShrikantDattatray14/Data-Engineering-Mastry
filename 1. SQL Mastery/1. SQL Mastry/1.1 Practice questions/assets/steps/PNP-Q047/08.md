# Q047 · Final output and units reconciliation

[![Previous](../previous.svg)](07.md) <kbd>Next</kbd> · **8 of 8**

The final report has two qualifying brands. Check the units against the five input order lines: 2 + 1 + 1 + 2 + 1 = 7. The output reports 4 + 3 = 7 units. Product 1's repeated line contributes twice to units and value, but only once to the distinct sold-product count.

|---:|---:|---:|
| 10 | 1 | 2 |

| 14 | 5 | 1 |

## Result · 🟪 2 rows

| 🟪 Brand | 🟩 products_listed | 🟦 products_sold | 🟦 units_sold | 🟪 list_price_value |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Nova | 3 | 2 | 4 | 50 |
| Unbranded | 3 | 2 | 3 | 16 |

**Units check:** input order-line units = 7; output units = 7. The result is reconciled.

<details>
<summary>Full SQLite query for this step</summary>

```sql
SELECT
    COALESCE(P.Brand, 'Unbranded') AS Brand,
    COUNT(DISTINCT P.ProductID) AS products_listed,
    COUNT(DISTINCT OD.ProductID) AS products_sold,
    COALESCE(SUM(OD.Quantity), 0) AS units_sold,
    COALESCE(SUM(P.Sale_Price * OD.Quantity), 0) AS list_price_value
FROM Products AS P
LEFT JOIN OrderDetails AS OD
    ON P.ProductID = OD.ProductID
GROUP BY COALESCE(P.Brand, 'Unbranded')
HAVING COUNT(DISTINCT P.ProductID) >= 3
ORDER BY list_price_value DESC;
```
