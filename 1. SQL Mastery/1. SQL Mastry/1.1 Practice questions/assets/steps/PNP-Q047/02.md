# Q047 · Why LEFT JOIN OrderDetails?

[![Previous](../previous.svg)](01.md) [![Next](../next.svg)](03.md) · **2 of 8**

🟦 OrderDetails supplies the order-line quantities. Use a LEFT JOIN because the catalogue row must survive even when there is no matching order line. Product 1 has two matching lines, so it appears twice after the join. Products 3, 6, 7 and 8 receive NULLs on the order-line side.

## SQL added or changed

```sql
SELECT P.ProductID, P.Brand, P.Sale_Price,
       OD.OrderDetailID, OD.ProductID AS sold_product_id, OD.Quantity
FROM Products AS P
LEFT JOIN OrderDetails AS OD
    ON P.ProductID = OD.ProductID
ORDER BY P.ProductID, OD.OrderDetailID;
```

## Input to this step

**🟦 OrderDetails · 5 rows**

| 🟦 OrderDetailID | 🟦 ProductID | 🟦 Quantity |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 10 | 1 | 2 |
| 11 | 1 | 1 |
| 12 | 2 | 1 |
| 13 | 4 | 2 |
| 14 | 5 | 1 |

## Result · 🟪 10 rows

| 🟩 ProductID | 🟩 Brand | 🟩 Sale_Price | 🟦 OrderDetailID | 🟦 sold_product_id | 🟦 Quantity |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | Nova | 10 | 10 | 1 | 2 |
| 1 | Nova | 10 | 11 | 1 | 1 |
| 2 | Nova | 20 | 12 | 2 | 1 |
| 3 | Nova | 30 | NULL | NULL | NULL |
| 4 | NULL | 5 | 13 | 4 | 2 |
| 5 | NULL | 6 | 14 | 5 | 1 |
| 6 | NULL | 7 | NULL | NULL | NULL |
| 7 | Solo | 8 | NULL | NULL | NULL |
| 8 | Solo | 9 | NULL | NULL | NULL |

The row count becomes ten, not eight, because product 1 matches two order lines. The four unsold products are still present as NULL-extended rows.
