# Q047 · Why start with Products?

<kbd>Previous</kbd> [![Next](../next.svg)](02.md) · **1 of 8**

The report is about the catalogue population: every listed product must contribute to its brand's count, even when it has never sold. Therefore 🟩 Products is the driving table. Starting from 🟦 OrderDetails would lose products with no sale line.

## SQL added or changed

```sql
SELECT ProductID, Brand, Sale_Price
FROM Products
ORDER BY ProductID;
```

## Input to this step

**🟩 Products · 8 rows**

| 🟩 ProductID | 🟩 Brand | 🟩 Sale_Price |
|---:|---|---:|
| 1 | Nova | 10 |
| 2 | Nova | 20 |
| 3 | Nova | 30 |
| 4 | NULL | 5 |
| 5 | NULL | 6 |
| 6 | NULL | 7 |
| 7 | Solo | 8 |
| 8 | Solo | 9 |

## Result · 🟩 8 rows

All eight catalogue products remain in the working population. Notice product 3, product 6, product 7 and product 8: they have no order line in the sample, but they still count as listed products.
