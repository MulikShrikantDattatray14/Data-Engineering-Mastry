# P034 · Preserve unmatched rows from both sources

[![Previous](../previous.svg)](01.md) [![Next](../next.svg)](03.md) · **2 of 6**

Add 🟦 OrderDetails second: its SupplierID supplies quantities and reveals IDs missing from the supplier master. FULL OUTER JOIN preserves unmatched rows on both sides. Alpha matches twice, so its supplier columns repeat; detail ID 4 / supplier 99 survives without a supplier row; Gamma survives without detail.

**SQL added**
```sql
FULL OUTER JOIN OrderDetails AS OD
 ON S.SupplierID = OD.SupplierID
```
**Input · 🟦 OrderDetails (4 rows)**

| OrderDetailID | SupplierID | Quantity |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 1 | 2 |
| 2 | 1 | 3 |
| 3 | 2 | 5 |
| 4 | 99 | 4 |

**Result · 5 joined rows**

| S.SupplierID | CompanyName | OD.OrderDetailID | OD.SupplierID | Quantity |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | Alpha | 1 | 1 | 2 |
| 1 | Alpha | 2 | 1 | 3 |
| 2 | Beta | 3 | 2 | 5 |
| NULL | NULL | 4 | 99 | 4 |
| 3 | Gamma | NULL | NULL | NULL |
