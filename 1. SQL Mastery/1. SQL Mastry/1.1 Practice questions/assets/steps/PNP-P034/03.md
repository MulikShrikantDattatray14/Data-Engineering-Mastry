# P034 · Resolve supplier identity and label

[![Previous](../previous.svg)](02.md) [![Next](../next.svg)](04.md) · **3 of 6**

COALESCE resolves the two possible identity columns and labels unmatched supplier IDs. This projection preserves all five rows.

**SQL added**
```sql
COALESCE(S.SupplierID, OD.SupplierID) AS SupplierID,
COALESCE(S.CompanyName, 'Unknown supplier') AS CompanyName
```
**Result · 5 rows**

| SupplierID | CompanyName | OrderDetailID | Quantity |
|---:|---|---:|---:|
| 1 | Alpha | 1 | 2 |
| 1 | Alpha | 2 | 3 |
| 2 | Beta | 3 | 5 |
| 99 | Unknown supplier | 4 | 4 |
| 3 | Gamma | NULL | NULL |

Gamma's detail NULLs remain; ID 99 gains a usable resolved identity and fallback label.
