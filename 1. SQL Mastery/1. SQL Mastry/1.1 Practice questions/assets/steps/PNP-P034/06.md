# P034 · Calculate shares and sort

[![Previous](../previous.svg)](05.md) <kbd>Next</kbd> · **6 of 6**

The exact learner query calculates units × 100 / 14, rounds to two decimals, returns 0.00 for a zero denominator and sorts descending by units. Alpha and Beta tie, so their relative order is not guaranteed.

**SQL added**
```sql
ROUND(units_supplied * 100.0 / total_quantity, 2)
ORDER BY units_supplied DESC
```
**Result · 4 rows**

| SupplierID | CompanyName | units_supplied | pct_of_units |
|---:|---|---:|---:|
| 1 | Alpha | 5 | 35.71 |
| 2 | Beta | 5 | 35.71 |
| 99 | Unknown supplier | 4 | 28.57 |
| 3 | Gamma | 0 | 0.00 |

Reconciliation: 5 + 5 + 4 + 0 = 14. Rounded shares sum to 99.99%; unrounded shares sum to 100%.
