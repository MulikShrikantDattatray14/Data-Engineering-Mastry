# P006 · Label and rank the full-price products

[![Previous](../previous.svg)](🟦 02.md) <kbd>Next</kbd>  · **3 of 3**

Every retained product gets the same label before sorting and limiting.

## SQL added or changed

```sql
SELECT ProductID, Product, Brand, Sale_Price, Market_Price,
       'Full price' AS discount_display
-- …
ORDER BY Sale_Price DESC, ProductID ASC
LIMIT 10;
```

## Input to this step

**Keep products with equal prices · 🟦 2 rows**

| 🟦 ProductID | 🟦 Product | 🟦 Brand | 🟦 Sale_Price | 🟦 Market_Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 🟦 1 | 🟦 Mug | 🟦 Home | 🟦 100 | 🟦 100 |
| 🟦 2 | 🟦 Lamp | 🟦 Home | 🟦 150 | 🟦 150 |

## Result · 🟦 2 rows

| 🟦 ProductID | 🟦 Product | 🟦 Brand | 🟦 Sale_Price | 🟦 Market_Price | 🟦 discount_display |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 🟦 2 | 🟦 Lamp | 🟦 Home | 🟦 150 | 🟦 150 | 🟦 Full price |
| 🟦 1 | 🟦 Mug | 🟦 Home | 🟦 100 | 🟦 100 | 🟦 Full price |

<details>
<summary>Full SQLite query for this step</summary>

```sql
SELECT ProductID, Product, Brand, Sale_Price, Market_Price,
       'Full price' AS discount_display
FROM Products
WHERE Market_Price = Sale_Price
ORDER BY Sale_Price DESC, ProductID ASC
LIMIT 10;
```

</details>
