# Bosscoder-1.01 · Keep only 2021 records

<kbd>Previous</kbd> [![Next](../next.svg)](02.md)  · **1 of 3**

The question asks about revenue *in 2021*. Other years cannot decide whether a customer qualifies.

## SQL added or changed

```sql
SELECT customer_id, year, revenue
FROM Customers
WHERE year = 2021
ORDER BY customer_id;
```
