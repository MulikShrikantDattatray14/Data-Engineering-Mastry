# P023 · Find the two eligible subcategories

<kbd>Previous</kbd> [![Next](../next.svg)](02.md)  · **1 of 3**

Percent signs are wildcards in LIKE. The substring cookie also matches 🟦 Cookies, and chocolate matches longer labels such as Dark 🟦 Chocolate. 🟦 Tea Biscuit drops out. Products is the only source because Product, Sub_Category and Sale_Price needed for the filters and result all belong to catalogue rows.

## SQL added or changed

```sql
select `ProductID`,Product,Sub_Category,Sale_Price
from Products
where ( trim(lower(Sub_Category)) like "%cookie%" or  trim(lower(Sub_Category)) like "%chocolate%")
order by ProductID;
```

## Input to this step

**🟦 Products · 🟦 4 rows**

| 🟦 ProductID | 🟦 Product | 🟦 Sub_Category | 🟦 Sale_Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 🟦 1 | 🟦 Cocoa Cookie | 🟦 Cookies | 🟦 200 |
| 🟦 2 | 🟦 Sugar-free Choc | 🟦 Chocolate | 🟦 150 |
| 🟦 3 | 🟦 Tea Biscuit | 🟦 Biscuits | 🟦 100 |
| 🟦 4 | 🟦 Dark Bar | 🟦 CHOCOLATE | 🟦 249 |
