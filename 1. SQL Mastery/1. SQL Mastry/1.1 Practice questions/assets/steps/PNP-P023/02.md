# P023 · Apply the price boundary

[![Previous](../previous.svg)](01.md) [![Next](../next.svg)](03.md)  · **2 of 3**

AND applies the price test to the entire parenthesized OR. All three remaining sample rows pass this check. A chocolate item priced exactly 250 fails.

## SQL added or changed

```sql
where ( trim(lower(Sub_Category)) like "%cookie%" or  trim(lower(Sub_Category)) like "%chocolate%")  and `Sale_Price` < 250
```

## Input to this step

**Find the two eligible subcategories · 3 rows**

| 🟦 ProductID | 🟦 Product | 🟦 Sub_Category | 🟦 Sale_Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Cocoa Cookie | Cookies | 200 |
| 2 | Sugar-free Choc | Chocolate | 150 |
| 4 | Dark Bar | CHOCOLATE | 249 |

## Result · 3 rows

| 🟦 ProductID | 🟦 Product | 🟦 Sub_Category | 🟦 Sale_Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Cocoa Cookie | Cookies | 200 |
| 2 | Sugar-free Choc | Chocolate | 150 |
| 4 | Dark Bar | CHOCOLATE | 249 |

<details>
<summary>Full SQLite query for this step</summary>

```sql
select `ProductID`,Product,Sub_Category,Sale_Price
from Products
where ( trim(lower(Sub_Category)) like "%cookie%" or  trim(lower(Sub_Category)) like "%chocolate%")  and `Sale_Price` < 250
order by ProductID;
```

</details>
