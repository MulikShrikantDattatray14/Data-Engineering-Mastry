# P012 · Preview the contribution of each product

[![Previous](../previous.svg)](🟦 02.md) [![Next](../next.svg)](04.md)  · **3 of 4**

Compare each product's own sale and market prices. Their valid discounts are 🟦 20% and 🟦 0%, so both contribute to the average.

## SQL added or changed

```sql
SELECT ProductID, CategoryID,
       CASE WHEN Sale_Price = Market_Price THEN 1 ELSE 0 END AS full_price_flag,
       CASE
           WHEN Market_Price IS NOT NULL AND Market_Price != 0
           THEN (Market_Price - Sale_Price) * 100.0 / Market_Price
       END AS product_discount_pct
-- …
ORDER BY ProductID;
```

## Input to this step

**🟦 Products · 🟦 2 rows**

| 🟦 ProductID | 🟦 CategoryID | 🟦 Sale_Price | 🟦 Market_Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 🟦 1 | 🟦 10 | 🟦 80 | 🟦 100 |
| 🟦 2 | 🟦 10 | 🟦 100 | 🟦 100 |

## Result · 🟦 2 rows

| 🟦 ProductID | 🟦 CategoryID | 🟦 full_price_flag | 🟦 product_discount_pct |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 🟦 1 | 🟦 10 | 🟦 0 | 🟦 20.0 |
| 🟦 2 | 🟦 10 | 🟦 1 | 🟦 0.0 |
