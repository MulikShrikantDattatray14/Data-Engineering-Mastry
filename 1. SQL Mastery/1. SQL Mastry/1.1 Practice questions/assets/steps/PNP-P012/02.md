# P012 · Add the sale-price statistics

[![Previous](../previous.svg)](01.md) [![Next](../next.svg)](03.md)  · **2 of 4**

Category 10 has sale prices of 80 and 100. The minimum is 80, the maximum is 100, and the mean is 90.

## SQL added or changed

```sql
SELECT CategoryID, COUNT(ProductID) AS products,
       MIN(Sale_Price) AS min_price,
       MAX(Sale_Price) AS max_price,
       ROUND(AVG(Sale_Price), 2) AS avg_price
```

## Input to this step

**🟦 Products · 2 rows**

| 🟦 ProductID | 🟦 CategoryID | 🟦 Sale_Price | 🟦 Market_Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 10 | 80 | 100 |
| 2 | 10 | 100 | 100 |

## Result · 1 row

| 🟦 CategoryID | 🟦 products | 🟦 min_price | 🟦 max_price | 🟦 avg_price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10 | 2 | 80 | 100 | 90.0 |

<details>
<summary>Full SQLite query for this step</summary>

```sql
SELECT CategoryID, COUNT(ProductID) AS products,
       MIN(Sale_Price) AS min_price,
       MAX(Sale_Price) AS max_price,
       ROUND(AVG(Sale_Price), 2) AS avg_price
FROM Products
GROUP BY CategoryID
ORDER BY CategoryID;
```
