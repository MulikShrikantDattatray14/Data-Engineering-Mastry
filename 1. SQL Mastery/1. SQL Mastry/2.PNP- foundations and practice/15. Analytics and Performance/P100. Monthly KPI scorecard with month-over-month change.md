# PNP Q100. Monthly KPI scorecard with month-over-month change

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![EXPERT](<../../_badges/expert.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 100, pp. 210–213. **Difficulty:** Expert.

**Business scenario.** Every month the leadership team gets one table: eight KPIs for the latest month next to the month before, with the change. The data extract rarely ends on a month end. This one ends on 28 December, so December is a partial month, and comparing 28 days of December with 30 days of November would show a fall that is only the calendar. The analytics lead wants the comparison to be fair and the choice visible on the page.

**Task.** Anchor on the latest OrderDate . Compare the latest month from day 1 to the anchor day against the same day range of the previous month (capped at that month's last day). For each period compute: orders, order value ( Total_Order_Amount ), average order value, active customers, new customers, repeat-customer order share, on-time delivery rate, and units. Return one row per KPI with both values, the absolute change, the percent change (counts and money only), and the two date windows. Keep the KPI order fixed.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
kpi , unit ( count , money or % )
prior_value , latest_value
change : latest_value - prior_value (percentage points for % rows)
change_pct : percent change for count and money rows; NULL for % rows
prior_window , latest_window : the exact dates compared
KPI definitions:
Orders: rows in Orders dated in the window. Cancellations and returns are not modelled.
Order value: SUM(Total_Order_Amount) , the net amount payable after the payment-linked discount.
Average order value: AVG(Total_Order_Amount) over the window's orders.
Active customers: distinct customers with an order in the window.
New customers: active customers whose first-ever order falls in the window.
Repeat-customer order share: share of the window's orders placed by customers whose first order was before the window started.
On-time delivery: share of delivered orders with DeliveryDate - OrderDate <= 7 (the house one-week promise). A delivery dated
before its order is a data error (Q88 rule 4) and is left out of both counts.
Units: SUM(Quantity) over the window's order lines.
```
