# PNP Q048. Do discounted products sell more?

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![MEDIUM](<../../_badges/medium.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 48, pp. 83–85. **Difficulty:** Medium.

**Business scenario.** The pricing team wants a bigger promotions budget. Their argument is that discounted products sell more, so deeper discounts will lift volume. Before approving it, the head of category wants a simple check: group products into discount bands and compare how many units an average product in each band has sold. "Sells more" means higher average units per product, with unsold products counted as zero.

**Task.** Band every product by its discount percentage, (Market_Price - Sale_Price) / Market_Price * 100 : Full price (0%), Up to 10% , 10% to 25% , Over 25% , and Unknown when Market_Price is missing or zero. For each band, return the number of products, how many of them ever sold, the average units sold per product (zero sellers included) and total units. List bands from full price to deepest discount, Unknown last.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
discount_band
products : products in the band, sold or not
products_sold : products in the band with at least one order line
avg_units_per_product : total units in the band divided by products in the band, 2 decimals
total_units : sum of Quantity for those products
```

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.

**Expected output**

| discount_band | products | products_sold | avg_units_per_product | total_units |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Full price | 1 | 1 | 2.00 | 2 |
| Up to 10% | 1 | 1 | 1.00 | 1 |
| 10% to 25% | 1 | 1 | 3.00 | 3 |
| Over 25% | 1 | 0 | 0.00 | 0 |
| Unknown | 1 | 1 | 4.00 | 4 |

Product 4 has no sales but remains in its band with zero units.
