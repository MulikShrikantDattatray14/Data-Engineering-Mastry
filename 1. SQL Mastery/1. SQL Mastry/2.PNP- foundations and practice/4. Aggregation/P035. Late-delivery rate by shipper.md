# PNP Q035. Late-delivery rate by shipper

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![MEDIUM](<../../_badges/medium.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 35, pp. 59–61. **Difficulty:** Medium.

**Business scenario.** The logistics team reviews courier performance every quarter and moves volume away from the worst performer. They want each shipper's late-delivery rate. There is no promised delivery date in the data, so the team has agreed a working rule: an order is late when it is delivered more than 7 days after it was placed.

**Task.** For each shipper, count delivered orders, late orders and the late rate. Count orders with no delivery date separately, and keep orders with impossible date sequences out of the rate but visible in their own column. Orders with a missing or unknown ShipperID go under Unknown shipper . Sort by late rate, highest first.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
shipper_name : CompanyName , or Unknown shipper
delivered_orders : orders with a valid delivery date (the rate's denominator)
late_orders : delivered orders with DeliveryDate - OrderDate > 7
late_rate_pct : late as a percentage of delivered (2 dp)
not_delivered : orders with no DeliveryDate
invalid_dates : orders excluded because their dates are out of sequence
```

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
