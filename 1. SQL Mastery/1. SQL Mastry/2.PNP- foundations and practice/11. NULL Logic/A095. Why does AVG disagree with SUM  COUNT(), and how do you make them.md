### Sample input and expected output (illustrative)

The PDF uses an `order_items.discount` field for this exercise, although `discount` is absent from its shared seven-table schema.

**Input — `order_items`**:

| item_id | discount |
|---:|---:|
| 1 | 10 |
| 2 | NULL |
| 3 | 20 |

**Expected output:** `AVG(discount)` ignores the NULL and gives 15. `SUM(discount) / COUNT(*)` divides 30 by all three rows and gives 10. Dividing by the count of non-NULL discounts gives 15 again.

| avg_discount | sum_div_all_rows | sum_div_nonnull_rows |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 15 | 10 | 15 |
