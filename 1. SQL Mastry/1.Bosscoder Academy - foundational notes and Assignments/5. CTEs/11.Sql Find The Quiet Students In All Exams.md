# 11. SQL Find the Quiet Students in All Exams

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![UNRATED](<../../_badges/unrated.svg>)

## Question and image

A quiet student took at least one exam and did not score the highest or the lowest score in any exam they took.

Write a solution to report the quiet students as (student_id, student_name). Do not return a student who never took an exam. Return the result ordered by student_id.

**Example 1**

Student:

| student_id | student_name |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Daniel |
| 2 | Jade |
| 3 | Stella |
| 4 | Jonathan |
| 5 | Will |

Exam:

| exam_id | student_id | score |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 10 | 1 | 70 |
| 10 | 2 | 80 |
| 10 | 3 | 90 |
| 20 | 1 | 80 |
| 30 | 1 | 70 |
| 30 | 3 | 80 |
| 30 | 4 | 90 |
| 40 | 1 | 60 |
| 40 | 2 | 70 |
| 40 | 4 | 80 |

Output:

| student_id | student_name |
|---:|---|
| 2 | Jade |

For exam 10, Daniel and Stella have the lowest and highest scores. Daniel has both the lowest and highest score in exam 20. Daniel and Jonathan have the lowest and highest scores in exams 30 and 40. Jade never has an extreme score. Will took no exam, so only Jade is returned.

## Schema

Student:

| Column Name | Type |
|---|---|
| student_id | int |
| student_name | varchar |

student_id is the primary key. student_name is the name of the student.

Exam:

| Column Name | Type |
|---|---|
| exam_id | int |
| student_id | int |
| score | int |

(exam_id, student_id) is the primary key. Each row records a student's score in one exam.
