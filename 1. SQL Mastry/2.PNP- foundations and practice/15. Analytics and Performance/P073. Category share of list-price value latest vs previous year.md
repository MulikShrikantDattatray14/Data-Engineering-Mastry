# PNP Q073. Category share of list-price value: latest vs previous year

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![HARD](<../../_badges/hard.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 73, pp. 134–136. **Difficulty:** Hard.

**Business scenario.** The category management team sets next year's shelf space and marketing budget per category. Almost every category grew last year because the whole business grew (Baby Care is the one exception), so growth figures do not tell them much. They want mix shift instead: what share of total sales value each category had in the previous year and in the latest year, and how many percentage points that share moved. A category that grew slower than the business lost share, even if its own sales went up.

**Task.** For the latest calendar year in the data and the year before it, compute each category's share of total list-price value ( Quantity * Sale_Price ) within that year. Return both shares and the change in percentage points. Products whose CategoryID has no row in Category go under Unmapped . Sort by the change, biggest gain first.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
CategoryName : category name, or Unmapped
prev_share_pct : share of the previous year's list-price value, 2 dp (0 if no sales that year)
latest_share_pct : share of the latest year's list-price value, 2 dp (0 if no sales that year)
share_change_pp : latest share minus previous share, in percentage points, 2 dp
```

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
