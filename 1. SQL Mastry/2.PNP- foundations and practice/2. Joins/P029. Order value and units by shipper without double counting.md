# PNP Q029. Order value and units by shipper without double counting

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![MEDIUM](<../../_badges/medium.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 29, pp. 49–50. **Difficulty:** Medium.

**Primary topic:** One-to-many joins (fan-out). **Also covers:** Pre-aggregation in a CTE, LEFT JOIN, COALESCE.

**Business scenario.** The logistics finance team is renegotiating courier rate cards. For each shipper they want three numbers side by side: how many orders it carried, the total order value it carried, and how many units were inside those orders. Last quarter an analyst produced this report and the order value came out higher than the finance ledger. Your job is to produce it correctly.

**Task.** For every shipper that carried at least one order, return the number of orders, the sum of Total_Order_Amount for those orders, and the total units ( Quantity ) in those orders. Orders with a missing or unrecognised ShipperID should appear under one row labelled Unknown shipper . Sort by order value, highest first.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
shipper_name : the shipper's CompanyName , or Unknown shipper
orders : number of orders carried
order_value : sum of Total_Order_Amount for those orders, each order counted once
units_shipped : sum of Quantity across the lines of those orders
```

**Tables required:** Orders , OrderDetails , Shippers.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** data grain, one-to-many fan-out, aggregating before joining, LEFT JOIN to keep unmatched keys, COALESCE for labels.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
