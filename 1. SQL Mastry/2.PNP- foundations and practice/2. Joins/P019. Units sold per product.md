# PNP Q019. Units sold per product

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![EASY](<../../_badges/easy.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 19, pp. 34–35. **Difficulty:** Easy.

**Primary topic:** JOIN with SUM at product grain. **Also covers:** Order lines vs units, COUNT DISTINCT.

**Business scenario.** Merchandising decides which products get the end-of-aisle display in the app. They look at three numbers per product: units sold, the number of order lines, and the number of distinct orders. A product bought in bulk by a few customers and one bought one at a time by many need different promotions. Cancellations and returns are not recorded here, so every order line counts as sold.

**Task.** For each product that appears in OrderDetails , return units sold, order lines and distinct orders. Sort by units sold, highest first, then ProductID .

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
ProductID
Product
units_sold : sum of Quantity
order_lines : rows in OrderDetails for the product
orders : distinct OrderID s containing the product
```

**Tables required:** OrderDetails , Products.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** table grain, INNER JOIN, SUM vs COUNT vs COUNT DISTINCT, GROUP BY.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
