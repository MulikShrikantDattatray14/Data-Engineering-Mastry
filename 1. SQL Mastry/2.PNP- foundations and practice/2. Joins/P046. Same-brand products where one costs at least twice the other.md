# PNP Q046. Same-brand products where one costs at least twice the other

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![MEDIUM](<../../_badges/medium.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 46, pp. 79–81. **Difficulty:** Medium.

**Primary topic:** Self join. **Also covers:** Non-equi join condition to orient pairs, Ratio with NULLIF.

**Business scenario.** The pricing team is reviewing price ladders. When one brand sells two products in the same sub-category and one costs at least twice the other, shoppers either see a clear good-better-best ladder or a gap with nothing in the middle. The team wants every such pair listed once, cheaper product on the left, so they can decide where a mid-priced pack is missing.

**Task.** Find every pair of products with the same Brand and the same Sub_Category where the pricier product's Sale_Price is at least 2 times the cheaper one's. Return each pair once. Products with no brand are not part of any brand ladder. Sort by price ratio, highest first.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
Brand
Sub_Category
cheaper_product , cheaper_price
pricier_product , pricier_price
price_ratio : pricier price divided by cheaper price, 2 decimals
```

**Tables required:** Products.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** self join with table aliases, equality plus inequality join conditions, mirrored-pair elimination, NULL behaviour in join keys.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.

**Expected output**

| Brand | Sub_Category | cheaper_product | cheaper_price | pricier_product | pricier_price | price_ratio |

| Nova | Tea | Small | 50 | Large | 300 | 6.00 |
| Nova | Tea | Medium | 120 | Large | 300 | 2.50 |
| Nova | Tea | Small | 50 | Medium | 120 | 2.40 |

Each qualifying pair appears once, with the cheaper product first.
