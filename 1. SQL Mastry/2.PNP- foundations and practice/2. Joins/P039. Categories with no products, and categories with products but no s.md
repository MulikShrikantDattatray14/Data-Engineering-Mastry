# PNP Q039. Categories with no products, and categories with products but no sales

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![MEDIUM](<../../_badges/medium.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 39, pp. 67–68. **Difficulty:** Medium.

**Business scenario.** The catalogue team runs a clean-up every half year. Some categories were created and never stocked; others have products that never sold. An empty category is deleted, while an unsold range is reviewed with the buyer. They want every category with its product count, how many of its products have sold, and a status naming the action.

**Task.** For every row in Category , return the number of products in it, how many of those products appear on at least one order line, the total units sold, and a status: No products , Products but no sales , Some products unsold or All products sold . Put the categories needing action first: empty categories, then the lowest share of products sold.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
CategoryID , CategoryName
product_count : products in the category
products_sold : products with at least one order line
units_sold : sum of Quantity (0 when none)
status : one of the four labels above
```
