# PNP Q053. Thirty-day sign-up to purchase conversion by country

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![MEDIUM](<../../_badges/medium.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 53, pp. 91–93. **Difficulty:** Medium.

**Primary topic:** LEFT JOIN with a per-customer aggregate. **Also covers:** MIN per group in a CTE, Conditional COUNT with FILTER, Percentage, Date difference.

**Business scenario.** The growth team compares onboarding across markets. "Converted at some point" hides how long people take, and most customers here do order eventually. The number they track is speed: the share of customers whose first order came within 30 days of signing up. They want it per country, so they can see where the onboarding flow is slow.

**Task.** For each Country , return the number of customers, how many placed their first order within 30 days of DateEntered (day 0 to day 30 inclusive), and that share as a percentage. Customers who never ordered count in the denominator and not in the numerator. Sort by conversion rate, lowest first, then by country.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
country
customers : customers registered in that country
converted_30d : customers whose first order is 0 to 30 days after sign-up
conversion_30d_pct : converted_30d as a percentage of customers , 2 decimals
```

**Tables required:** Customers , Orders.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** reducing Orders to one row per customer before joining, LEFT JOIN so non-buyers stay in the denominator, date difference in days, COUNT(*) FILTER , percentage with NULLIF .

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
