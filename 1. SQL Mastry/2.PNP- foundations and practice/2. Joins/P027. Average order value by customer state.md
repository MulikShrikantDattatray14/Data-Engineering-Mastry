# PNP Q027. Average order value by customer state

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 27, pp. 45–47. **Difficulty:** Medium.

**Primary topic:** JOIN with GROUP BY and HAVING. **Also covers:** AOV on non-null totals, LEFT JOIN, COALESCE labels.

**Business scenario.** The regional marketing team wants to raise the free-delivery threshold in states where baskets are already large. They need average order value (AOV) per customer state, but a state with a handful of orders gives a number nobody should act on. They have agreed to look only at states with at least 10 orders.

**Task.** For each customer state, return the number of distinct ordering customers, the number of orders, the total order value and the average order value. Group orders whose customer has no State , or whose CustomerID matches no customer, under Unknown . Keep only states with 10 or more orders. Sort by AOV, highest first.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
state : the customer's State , or Unknown
customers : distinct customers who placed orders
orders : number of orders
order_value : sum of Total_Order_Amount
avg_order_value : order value divided by the number of orders that have a total
```

**Tables required:** Orders , Customers.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** LEFT JOIN to keep unmatched orders, COALESCE and NULLIF for labels, GROUP BY with HAVING, AOV built from SUM and COUNT, COUNT DISTINCT.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
