# PNP Q038. Products from inactive categories that still sold in the latest year

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![MEDIUM](<../../_badges/medium.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 38, pp. 65–66. **Difficulty:** Medium.

**Primary topic:** Multi-join aggregation with a business-rule filter. **Also covers:** Anchored latest year, Flag normalisation, CTE.

**Business scenario.** The catalogue governance team switches categories off when the business exits them. A switched-off category should stop selling, but the storefront hides products by category only on some pages. The team wants every product in an inactive category that still appeared in orders during the latest calendar year in the data, with units sold and the last order date, so they can close each gap.

**Task.** Find the latest calendar year in Orders from the data itself. For every product whose category is not active and that had order lines in that year, return the units sold in that year and the most recent order date. Sort by units, highest first.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
ProductID , Product
CategoryName
units_latest_year : sum of Quantity on orders placed in the latest year
last_order_date : latest OrderDate for the product in that year
```

**Tables required:** Products , Category , OrderDetails , Orders.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** four-table join, anchoring a date window to the data, normalised flag test, filtering before aggregating, half-open year range.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.

### Sample input and expected output

**Input — `Category`**

| CategoryID | CategoryName | Active |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Old stock | 0 |
| 2 | Current stock | 1 |

**Input — `Products`**

| ProductID | Product | CategoryID |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | Old mug | 1 |
| 20 | New mug | 2 |

**Input — `Orders`**

| OrderID | OrderDate |
|---|---|
| 100 | 2025-01-01 |
| 101 | 2024-01-01 |

**Input — `OrderDetails`**

| OrderDetailID | OrderID | ProductID | Quantity |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 100 | 10 | 2 |
| 2 | 101 | 10 | 3 |
| 3 | 100 | 20 | 5 |

**Expected output**

| ProductID | Product | CategoryName | units_latest_year | last_order_date |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | Old mug | Old stock | 2 | 2025-01-01 |

The latest year is 2025; the older sale and the active-category product do not qualify.
