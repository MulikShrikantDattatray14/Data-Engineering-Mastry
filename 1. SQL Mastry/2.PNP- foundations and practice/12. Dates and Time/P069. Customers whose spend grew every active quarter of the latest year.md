# PNP Q069. Customers whose spend grew every active quarter of the latest year

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![HARD](<../../_badges/hard.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 69, pp. 125–127. **Difficulty:** Hard.

**Business scenario.** Account managers want a short list of "rising" customers to call before annual planning: people whose spending went up every time they came back during the latest year. A customer who grew strongly in one quarter and fell in another is not on the list. To keep it meaningful, the customer must have been active in at least three quarters of that year.

**Task.** Take the calendar year of the latest order date in the data. For each customer, sum Total_Order_Amount per quarter in which they ordered (an active quarter). Return customers with at least three active quarters whose spend in each active quarter was strictly higher than in their previous active quarter, with their first and last quarter spend.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
CustomerID
active_quarters : quarters of the latest year with at least one order (3 or 4)
first_quarter_spend : spend in the customer's first active quarter of the year
last_quarter_spend : spend in their last active quarter of the year
```

| CustomerID | active_quarters | first_quarter_spend | last_quarter_spend |
