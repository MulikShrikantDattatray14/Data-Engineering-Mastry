# PNP Q064. Three-month moving average of order value, with no missing months

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![HARD](<../../_badges/hard.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 64, pp. 113–116. **Difficulty:** Hard.

**Primary topic:** Moving averages. **Also covers:** generate_series month spine, LEFT JOIN to fill gaps, Explicit ROWS frame, Multiple CTEs.

**Business scenario.** Finance presents monthly order value to the board with a three-month moving average beside it, so one festive month or one slow month does not swing the story. Last quarter a month with no orders was simply missing from the chart, and the "three-month" average quietly averaged across four calendar months. They want a version that cannot do that.

**Task.** For every calendar month from the first order month to the latest order month in the data, return the total order value (0 for months with no orders) and the average of that month and the two before it. Return NULL for the moving average until three months are available.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
order_month : first day of the month, every month in the range, no gaps
order_value : sum of Total_Order_Amount in that month, 0 when there were no orders
moving_avg_3m : average of order_value over this month and the previous two, 2 dp; NULL for the first two months
```

**Tables required:** Orders.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** calendar spine with generate_series , LEFT JOIN to keep empty periods, COALESCE to zero, ROWS BETWEEN 2 PRECEDING AND CURRENT ROW , a named WINDOW , suppressing incomplete windows with COUNT(*) OVER .

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
