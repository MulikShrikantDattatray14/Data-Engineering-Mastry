# PNP Q003. Customers in metro cities

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![EASY](<../../_badges/easy.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 3, pp. 14–15. **Difficulty:** Easy.

**Primary topic:** IN list filtering. **Also covers:** TRIM and LOWER on dirty text, ORDER BY.

**Business scenario.** Marketing is launching same-day delivery in five metros: Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad and Chennai. They need the customers in those cities for the launch campaign. City was typed in by hand at sign-up, so some values carry stray spaces or odd capitalisation.

**Task.** Return customers whose city is one of the five metros, matching regardless of case and surrounding spaces. Show the city with the spaces removed. Sort by city, then CustomerID .

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
CustomerID
FirstName
LastName
City : the city with leading and trailing spaces removed
State
```

**Tables required:** Customers.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** IN lists, TRIM and LOWER for text comparison, ORDER BY.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
