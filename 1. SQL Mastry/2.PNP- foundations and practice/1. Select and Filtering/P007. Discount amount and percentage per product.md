# PNP Q007. Discount amount and percentage per product

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![EASY](<../../_badges/easy.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 7, pp. 19–20. **Difficulty:** Easy.

**Primary topic:** Arithmetic expressions. **Also covers:** NULLIF to avoid division by zero, ROUND, NULLS LAST.

**Business scenario.** The pricing team is checking how deep discounts run across the catalogue. Market_Price is the MRP printed on the pack and Sale_Price is what the store charges today. A missing or zero Market_Price means the MRP was never captured, so that product has no measurable discount.

**Task.** For every product, return the discount in rupees and as a percentage of Market_Price , rounded to two decimals. Products with a missing or zero Market_Price get NULL for both. Sort by discount percentage, highest first, with NULLs at the end.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
ProductID
Product
Market_Price
Sale_Price
discount_amount : Market_Price - Sale_Price
discount_pct : discount as a percentage of Market_Price , 2 decimals
```

**Tables required:** Products.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** column arithmetic, NULLIF, safe division, ROUND, NULLS LAST.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
