# PNP Q072. Same product on several lines of one order: split or duplicate?

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![HARD](<../../_badges/hard.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 72, pp. 132–134. **Difficulty:** Hard.

**Primary topic:** Window functions over a grouped key (ROW_NUMBER, COUNT, SUM OVER). **Also covers:** GROUP BY HAVING to find repeated keys, COUNT DISTINCT for classification, CASE verdicts, Deduplication design.

**Business scenario.** The data platform team is adding a uniqueness rule to OrderDetails : one line per product per order. Before switching it on, they need to know which existing orders break it and why. A product can appear twice in one order for a legitimate reason (the units were sourced from two different suppliers) or because the line was entered twice and should be consolidated. The team wants every offending line, the total quantity per product, and a verdict per group, so the clean-up script only merges what should be merged.

**Task.** Find every (OrderID, ProductID) that appears on more than one line. For each such line return its details, its position within the group (by OrderDetailID ), the group's line count and total quantity, and a verdict: Split across suppliers when the group's lines come from more than one SupplierID , otherwise Same supplier: consolidate . Add an action per line: for a split group every line is keep ; for a same-supplier group the first line is keep, set quantity to total and the others are remove . Sort by OrderID , ProductID , line position.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
OrderDetailID , OrderID , ProductID , Quantity , SupplierID
line_number : 1, 2, ... within the group, by OrderDetailID
lines_in_group , group_quantity : count of lines and sum of Quantity for that order-product
verdict , action
```

**Tables required:** OrderDetails.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** GROUP BY HAVING to find keys that repeat, COUNT(DISTINCT) to classify a group, window functions to attach group totals and positions to each row without collapsing it, deterministic ordering for a delete/keep decision.
