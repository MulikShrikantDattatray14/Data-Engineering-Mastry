# PNP Q071. Deduplicate customer accounts by email

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![HARD](<../../_badges/hard.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 71, pp. 130–132. **Difficulty:** Hard.

**Primary topic:** Deduplication with window functions. **Also covers:** Normalised match key, Deterministic tie-break, Self join to the survivor row.

**Business scenario.** The data platform team is building a customer master table. Some people have registered more than once with the same email, typed with different capitalisation or a stray space. Before the merge job runs, the team wants a mapping table they can review: for every duplicate account, which account survives and which one gets merged into it. The rule is that the earliest registration survives.

**Task.** Treat two accounts as the same person when their emails match after trimming spaces and lower-casing. Within each group of matching accounts, the survivor is the one with the earliest DateEntered ; if two accounts registered on the same day, the lower CustomerID survives. Return one row per non-surviving account. Accounts with a NULL or blank email are never matched.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
normalized_email : LOWER(TRIM(Email))
survivor_customer_id : the account that is kept
duplicate_customer_id : an account to merge into the survivor
survivor_date_entered , duplicate_date_entered : their registration dates
```

**Tables required:** Customers.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** normalising a match key, ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY key ORDER BY ...) , deterministic tie-breaking with NULLS LAST , joining a ranked set to itself to pair each duplicate with its survivor.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
