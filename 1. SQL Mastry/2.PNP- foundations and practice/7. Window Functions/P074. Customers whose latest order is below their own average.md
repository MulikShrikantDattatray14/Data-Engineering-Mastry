# PNP Q074. Customers whose latest order is below their own average

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![HARD](<../../_badges/hard.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 74, pp. 136–139. **Difficulty:** Hard.

**Primary topic:** Window frames (ROWS BETWEEN UNBOUNDED PRECEDING AND 1 PRECEDING). **Also covers:** ROW_NUMBER for the latest row, Named WINDOW clause, Anchored date filter, Percentage with NULLIF.

**Business scenario.** The CRM team runs a "basket shrink" nudge: a voucher for customers who are still buying but whose most recent basket was smaller than usual for them. "Smaller than usual" means the latest order's Total_Order_Amount is below the mean of that customer's earlier orders. A customer needs at least three earlier orders before the team trusts that mean. Customers whose latest order is more than 90 days old belong to the lapsed-customer campaign, not this one.

**Task.** For each customer, compare the latest order with the average of all orders placed before it. Return customers who have at least three earlier orders with an amount, whose latest order falls in the last 90 days of the data, and whose latest amount is below their prior average. Sort by the size of the drop, largest first.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
CustomerID
latest_order_date : date of the customer's most recent order
latest_amount : Total_Order_Amount of that order
prior_orders : number of earlier orders that have an amount (at least 3)
avg_prior_amount : mean amount of those earlier orders, 2 decimals
pct_below_avg : how far the latest amount sits below that mean, as a percentage of the mean
```

**Tables required:** Orders.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** explicit window frame ending at 1 PRECEDING , named WINDOW clause, descending ROW_NUMBER to pick the latest row, deterministic tie-breaks, anchored date filter, percentage with NULLIF .

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
