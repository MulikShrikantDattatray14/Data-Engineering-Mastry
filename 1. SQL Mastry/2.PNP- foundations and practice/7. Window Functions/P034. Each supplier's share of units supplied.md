# PNP Q034. Each supplier's share of units supplied

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![MEDIUM](<../../_badges/medium.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 34, pp. 58–59. **Difficulty:** Medium.

**Primary topic:** Window function over an aggregate. **Also covers:** FULL OUTER JOIN to keep both unmatched sides, Percentage contribution.

**Business scenario.** The supply chain team is reviewing dependence on each supplier before annual contracts are renewed. For every supplier on file they want the units it fulfilled and its share of all units. Two edge cases matter: a supplier with no lines must still appear with zero (it may be a contract to drop), and lines tagged with a SupplierID that is not on file must appear too, so the shares add up to 100.

**Task.** For every supplier, and for every unknown SupplierID found on order lines, return the units supplied and the share of all units. Suppliers with no lines show 0 units. Sort by units, highest first.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
SupplierID : the supplier's ID, or the unrecognised ID from the lines
CompanyName : the supplier's name, or Unknown supplier
units_supplied : sum of Quantity (0 when none)
pct_of_units : share of all units (2 dp)
```

**Tables required:** OrderDetails , Suppliers.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** window function over a grouped result ( SUM(SUM(...)) OVER () ), FULL OUTER JOIN, COALESCE on join keys, percentage of total.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
