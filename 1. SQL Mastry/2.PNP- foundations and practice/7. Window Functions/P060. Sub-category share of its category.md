# PNP Q060. Sub-category share of its category

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![MEDIUM](<../../_badges/medium.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 60, pp. 104–106. **Difficulty:** Medium.

**Primary topic:** Window SUM with PARTITION BY over aggregates. **Also covers:** GROUP BY at sub-category grain, LEFT JOIN for unmapped category, Percentage.

**Business scenario.** Each category manager splits shelf space and promotion slots among the sub-categories in their area. To do that fairly they want to know what share of the category's value each sub-category brings in. A sub-category's share is measured against its own category, not against the whole store, so every category's shares add up to 100.

**Task.** For every category and sub-category, return the list-price value of order lines and the sub-category's percentage of its category's list-price value. Products whose CategoryID has no row in Category are grouped under Unmapped . Sort by category name, then share, highest first.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
CategoryName , or Unmapped
Sub_Category , or Unknown
list_price_value : SUM(Quantity * Sale_Price) for the sub-category
pct_of_category : share of the category's list-price value, 2 decimals
```

**Tables required:** OrderDetails , Products , Category.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** grouping at a fine grain, then computing a coarser total with a window function over the grouped rows, PARTITION BY for per-group totals, contribution percentages.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
