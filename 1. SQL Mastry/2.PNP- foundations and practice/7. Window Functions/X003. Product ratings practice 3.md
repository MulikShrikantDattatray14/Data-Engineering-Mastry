**Task.** Within each sub-category, the products rated above the sub-category's average rating but selling below its median units.

The README provides no sample rows or expected-output table for this extra task. No separate question image was supplied.

### Sample input and expected output (illustrative)

**Input — `Products`**:

| ProductID | Product | Sub_Category | Rating |
|---:|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Item A | Headphones | 5 |
| 2 | Item B | Headphones | 3 |
| 3 | Item C | Headphones | 4 |

**Input — `OrderDetails`**:

| OrderDetailID | ProductID | Quantity |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 11 | 1 | 1 |
| 12 | 2 | 5 |
| 13 | 3 | 3 |

The Headphones average rating is `(5 + 3 + 4) / 3 = 4`; the median units sold per product among `1, 3, 5` is 3.

**Expected output** (only Item A is rated above 4 and sold below 3 units):

| ProductID | Product | Rating | units_sold |
|---:|---|---:|---:|
| 1 | Item A | 5 | 1 |
