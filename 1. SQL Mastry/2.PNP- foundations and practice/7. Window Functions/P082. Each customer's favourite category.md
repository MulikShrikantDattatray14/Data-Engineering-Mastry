# PNP Q082. Each customer's favourite category

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 82, pp. 156–158. **Difficulty:** Hard.

**Primary topic:** ROW_NUMBER over aggregates with a deterministic tie-break. **Also covers:** Multiple CTEs, Window SUM for share of customer units, LEFT JOIN for unmapped products, Multi-table join.

**Business scenario.** The personalisation team is building the home-page banner. Each customer sees one category, their favourite, and the banner system accepts exactly one category per customer. "Favourite" means the category in which the customer bought the most units across all their orders. The team also wants to know how dominant that category is, because a favourite that holds 25% of a customer's units is a weaker signal than one that holds 80%.

**Task.** For every customer who has order lines, return exactly one favourite category: the one with the most units. Break ties by the number of the customer's orders that contained the category, then by the most recent order date in that category, then alphabetically by category name. Show the units in that category and their share of the customer's total units. Lines whose product or category is missing go into a category labelled Unmapped .

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
CustomerID
favourite_category : CategoryName , or Unmapped
units_in_category : SUM(Quantity) in that category
pct_of_customer_units : that as a percentage of the customer's total units
```

**Tables required:** Orders , OrderDetails , Products , Category.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** aggregating to customer-category grain before ranking, ROW_NUMBER with a business tie-break, window SUM over the same partition for a share, LEFT JOINs to keep unmapped products, NULL quantities in ranking.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
