# PNP Q049. Top three products by units in each category

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![MEDIUM](<../../_badges/medium.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 49, pp. 85–86. **Difficulty:** Medium.

**Primary topic:** DENSE_RANK top-N per group. **Also covers:** Aggregation in a CTE, Filtering on a window result, LEFT JOIN for unmapped category.

**Business scenario.** Category managers are choosing which products get the three "Bestseller" badges on each category page. They want the top three products per category by units sold. If two products sell the same number of units, both should get the badge rather than one being dropped on an arbitrary tie-break, even if that means a category shows four badges.

**Task.** For each category, rank products by total units sold across all order lines and return every product whose rank is 1, 2 or 3, keeping ties. Products whose CategoryID has no row in Category are grouped under Unmapped . Only products with at least one order line take part. Sort by category name, then rank, then product ID.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
CategoryName : category name, or Unmapped
product_rank : 1, 2 or 3; tied products share a rank
ProductID , Product
units_sold : total Quantity for the product
```

**Tables required:** OrderDetails , Products , Category.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** aggregation before ranking, DENSE_RANK with PARTITION BY, why a window result needs a CTE before it can be filtered, LEFT JOIN for unmatched categories.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.

### Sample input and expected output

**Input — `Category`**

| CategoryID | CategoryName |
|---|---|
| 1 | Drinks |
| 2 | Snacks |

**Input — `Products`**

| ProductID | Product | CategoryID |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tea | 1 |
| 2 | Coffee | 1 |
| 3 | Juice | 1 |
| 4 | Water | 1 |
| 5 | Cookie | 2 |

**Input — `OrderDetails`**

| OrderDetailID | ProductID | Quantity |
|---|---|---|
| 11 | 1 | 10 |
| 12 | 2 | 8 |
| 13 | 3 | 8 |
| 14 | 4 | 1 |
| 15 | 5 | 2 |

**Expected output**

| CategoryName | product_rank | ProductID | Product | units_sold |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Drinks | 1 | 1 | Tea | 10 |
| Drinks | 2 | 2 | Coffee | 8 |
| Drinks | 2 | 3 | Juice | 8 |
| Drinks | 3 | 4 | Water | 1 |
| Snacks | 1 | 5 | Cookie | 2 |

The two products with eight units share rank 2. The next distinct unit total has dense rank 3.
