# PNP Q030. Repeat versus one-time customers

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 30, pp. 51–52. **Difficulty:** Medium.

**Primary topic:** Two-level aggregation with a CTE. **Also covers:** CASE buckets, Percentage of total.

**Business scenario.** The growth team is deciding whether next quarter's budget goes to acquiring new buyers or to a second-order nudge campaign. The first number they ask for is how many customers ever came back. A repeat customer here means a customer with two or more orders in the whole history; a one-time customer has exactly one.

**Task.** Classify every customer who has placed at least one order as One-time (exactly one order) or Repeat (two or more). Return the number of customers in each group and the group's share of all ordering customers.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
customer_type : One-time or Repeat
customers : number of customers in the group
pct_of_customers : share of all ordering customers (2 dp)
```

**Tables required:** Orders.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** aggregating twice (orders per customer, then customers per bucket), CTE read twice, CASE on an aggregated column, percentage of total.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
