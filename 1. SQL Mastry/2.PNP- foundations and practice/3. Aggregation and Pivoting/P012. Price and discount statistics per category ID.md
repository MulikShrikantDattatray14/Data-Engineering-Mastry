# PNP Q012. Price and discount statistics per category ID

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 12, pp. 25–27. **Difficulty:** Easy.

**Primary topic:** GROUP BY with several aggregates. **Also covers:** ROUND, Conditional COUNT with FILTER, AVG and NULLs.

**Business scenario.** The pricing team is preparing a quarterly review of category price ranges. For each category code they want the size of the range, the price spread, and how much of it is sold below the printed price ( Market_Price ), so they can spot categories that lean too hard on discounts.

**Task.** For each CategoryID in Products , return the number of products, the lowest, highest and average Sale_Price , how many products sell at full price ( Sale_Price = Market_Price ), and the average discount percentage across the category. Round averages to two decimals. Sort by CategoryID .

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
CategoryID
products : number of products
min_price , max_price : lowest and highest Sale_Price
avg_price : average Sale_Price , 2 decimals
full_price_products : products whose Sale_Price equals Market_Price
avg_discount_pct : average of (Market_Price - Sale_Price) / Market_Price * 100 , 2 decimals, ignoring products with no usable
Market_Price
```

**Tables required:** Products.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** GROUP BY, several aggregates in one query, ROUND, COUNT with FILTER, how AVG treats NULL.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
