# PNP Q025. Customer sign-ups by year and quarter

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![EASY](<../../_badges/easy.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 25, pp. 42–44. **Difficulty:** Easy.

**Primary topic:** Date-part grouping. **Also covers:** ORDER BY, NULL dates.

**Business scenario.** The growth team reports new-customer acquisition to the board every quarter. A sign-up is the day an account was created ( DateEntered ), whether or not the person has ordered. They want new accounts per year and quarter in time order, with undated accounts on their own line.

**Task.** Count customers by the year and quarter of DateEntered . Accounts with no DateEntered form one group with NULL year and quarter, listed last. Sort by year, then quarter.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
signup_year : year of DateEntered , as an integer
signup_quarter : quarter 1 to 4, as an integer
new_customers : accounts created in that quarter
```

**Tables required:** Customers.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** EXTRACT, grouping by two derived columns, casting, NULL groups, ORDER BY with NULLS LAST.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.

### Sample input and expected output

*Illustrative example; the source gives output columns but no sample rows. Only the columns needed for this example are shown.*

**Input — `Customers`**

| CustomerID | DateEntered |
|---|---|
| 1 | 2025-01-15 |
| 2 | 2025-03-01 |
| 3 | 2025-04-01 |
| 4 | NULL |

**Expected output**

| signup_year | signup_quarter | new_customers |
|---|---|---|
| 2025 | 1 | 2 |
| 2025 | 2 | 1 |
| NULL | NULL | 1 |

The missing sign-up date becomes one NULL year/quarter group, listed last.
