# PNP Q059. Basket size by month

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 59, pp. 102–103. **Difficulty:** Medium.

**Primary topic:** Two-level aggregation (per order, then per month). **Also covers:** Pre-aggregation CTE, Orders without lines, DATE_TRUNC.

**Business scenario.** Merchandising launched "add-on" suggestions at checkout and wants to know whether baskets got bigger. They track two numbers per month: the average number of units in an order and the average number of different products in an order. An order with no lines on record tells nothing about basket size and should be left out, but the team wants to see how many orders were measured each month.

**Task.** For each month, return the number of orders that have at least one line, the average units per order and the average distinct products per order. Months are taken from OrderDate . Sort by month.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
order_month : first day of the month
orders_with_lines : orders that month with at least one line in OrderDetails
avg_units_per_order : average of each order's total Quantity , 2 decimals
avg_distinct_products_per_order : average of each order's distinct ProductID count, 2 decimals
```

**Tables required:** Orders , OrderDetails.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** averaging at the right grain, aggregating lines to orders before averaging, COUNT DISTINCT per order, the difference between per-line and per-order averages.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
