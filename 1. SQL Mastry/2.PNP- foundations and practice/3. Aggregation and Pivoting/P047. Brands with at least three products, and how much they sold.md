# PNP Q047. Brands with at least three products, and how much they sold

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![MEDIUM](<../../_badges/medium.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 47, pp. 81–82. **Difficulty:** Medium.

**Primary topic:** COUNT DISTINCT with HAVING across a join. **Also covers:** LEFT JOIN to keep unsold products, List-price value.

**Business scenario.** The brand management team is choosing which brands get a dedicated brand page on the app. A brand qualifies only if it lists at least three products, because a page with one or two items looks empty. For each qualifying brand they also want to see how much of the range actually sells, so they can push brands to fix dead listings before the page goes live.

**Task.** For every brand with three or more products in Products , return how many products it lists, how many of those have at least one order line, total units sold and list-price value. Products with no brand are grouped under Unbranded . Sort by list-price value, highest first.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
Brand : brand name, or Unbranded
products_listed : distinct products of the brand in the catalogue
products_sold : distinct products with at least one order line
units_sold : total Quantity , 0 if nothing sold
list_price_value : SUM(Quantity * Sale_Price) , 0 if nothing sold
```

**Tables required:** Products , OrderDetails.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** COUNT vs COUNT DISTINCT after a one-to-many join, HAVING on a distinct count, LEFT JOIN to keep zero-sale rows, COALESCE.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
