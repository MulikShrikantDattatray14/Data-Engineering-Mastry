# PNP Q054. Weighted vs simple average discount by sub-category

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 54, pp. 93–95. **Difficulty:** Medium.

**Primary topic:** Weighted average (SUM / SUM). **Also covers:** Pre-aggregation with LEFT JOIN, Validity filter on Market_Price, Multiple CTEs.

**Business scenario.** The pricing team publishes an "average discount" per sub-category in its monthly deck. The merchandising head has challenged it: a deep discount on a product nobody buys counts as much as a small discount on the best seller, so the number does not describe what shoppers actually get. The team wants both views side by side: the simple average across products, and the average weighted by units sold.

**Task.** For each Sub_Category , compute each product's discount percentage, (Market_Price - Sale_Price) / Market_Price * 100 , using only products with a valid price pair ( Market_Price > 0 and Sale_Price not above Market_Price ). Return the number of products, the simple average discount, total units sold, and the unit-weighted average discount. Show the sub-categories where the two averages differ most first.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
Sub_Category , or Unknown
products : valid products in the sub-category, sold or not
simple_avg_discount_pct : plain average of product discount percentages, 2 decimals
units_sold : total units of those products
unit_weighted_discount_pct : SUM(discount_pct * units) / SUM(units) , 2 decimals
```

**Tables required:** Products , OrderDetails.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** weighted average as a ratio of sums, pre-aggregating lines per product, LEFT JOIN with zero units, filtering out invalid prices before averaging.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
