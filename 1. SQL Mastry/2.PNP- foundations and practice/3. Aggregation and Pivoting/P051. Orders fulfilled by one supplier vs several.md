# PNP Q051. Orders fulfilled by one supplier vs several

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 51, pp. 88–90. **Difficulty:** Medium.

**Primary topic:** Two-level aggregation with bucketing. **Also covers:** COUNT DISTINCT per order, CASE buckets, Percentage of total with a window over an aggregate.

**Business scenario.** The fulfilment team pays a handling cost every time an order has to be consolidated from more than one supplier's stock. Before redesigning warehouse slotting, they want to know how common split orders are. "Supplier mix" means the number of different suppliers across an order's lines.

**Task.** For every order in OrderDetails , count the distinct suppliers across its lines. Bucket orders as Single supplier , 2 suppliers or 3+ suppliers , and use No supplier recorded when all of an order's lines have a NULL SupplierID . For each bucket, return the number of orders and its share of all orders. Sort from fewest suppliers to most.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
supplier_mix : the bucket label
orders : orders in the bucket
pct_of_orders : share of all orders with lines, 2 decimals
```

**Tables required:** OrderDetails.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** aggregating lines to the order grain, COUNT DISTINCT, CASE over an aggregate, a second GROUP BY on buckets, SUM(COUNT(*)) OVER () for the total.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
