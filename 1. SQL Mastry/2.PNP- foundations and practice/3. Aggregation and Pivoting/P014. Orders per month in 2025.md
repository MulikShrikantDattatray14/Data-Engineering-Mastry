# PNP Q014. Orders per month in 2025

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![EASY](<../../_badges/easy.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 14, pp. 28–29. **Difficulty:** Easy.

**Primary topic:** DATE_TRUNC grouping. **Also covers:** Date range filter.

**Business scenario.** Operations plans warehouse staffing month by month and wants to see how 2025 actually went: how many orders each month and the order value they carried. Cancellations and refunds are not recorded in this dataset, so every row in Orders counts as a placed order.

**Task.** For each month of 2025, return the first day of the month, the number of orders and the total Total_Order_Amount . Sort by month.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
order_month : first day of the month, as a date
orders : orders placed that month
order_value : sum of Total_Order_Amount
```

**Tables required:** Orders.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** DATE_TRUNC, grouping by a derived value, half-open date filter, SUM and NULL.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
