# PNP Q021. Top five customers by number of orders

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 21, pp. 37–38. **Difficulty:** Easy.

**Primary topic:** GROUP BY with ORDER BY and LIMIT. **Also covers:** FETCH FIRST ... WITH TIES, LEFT JOIN for names.

**Business scenario.** The CRM team is sending thank-you hampers to its most frequent buyers, meaning the most orders placed, regardless of value. The budget covers five, but if customers tie for fifth place, the team would rather send an extra hamper than leave one out.

**Task.** Return the customers with the five highest order counts, including everyone tied at fifth place. Show Unknown customer when the CustomerID on the orders has no match in Customers . Sort by orders placed, highest first, then CustomerID .

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
CustomerID
customer_name : first and last name, or Unknown customer
orders_placed : number of orders
```

**Tables required:** Orders , Customers.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** GROUP BY, ORDER BY with LIMIT, FETCH FIRST ... WITH TIES, LEFT JOIN, table grain.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
