# PNP Q055. Category list-price value: latest year vs previous year

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 55, pp. 95–97. **Difficulty:** Medium.

**Primary topic:** Conditional aggregation by year. **Also covers:** Anchored year boundaries, Multi-table join, Growth percentage.

**Business scenario.** Category managers are setting next year's targets and want one table: each category's value last year, this year, and the growth between them. The table will be refreshed on whatever extract the analytics team receives, so "this year" cannot be hard-coded. It means the calendar year of the most recent order in the data, and "previous year" is the calendar year before it.

**Task.** For each category, return the list-price value of order lines in the previous calendar year and in the latest calendar year, plus year-over-year growth as a percentage. The latest year is the year of MAX(OrderDate) . Products whose CategoryID has no Category row are reported as Unmapped . Sort by growth, highest first.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
CategoryName , or Unmapped
prev_year_value : list-price value in the previous calendar year, 0 if none
latest_year_value : list-price value in the latest calendar year, 0 if none
yoy_growth_pct : (latest - previous) / previous * 100 , 2 decimals, NULL when previous is 0
```

**Tables required:** Orders , OrderDetails , Products , Category.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** anchoring dates to the data, half-open year ranges, conditional aggregation with FILTER to pivot two periods into columns, growth with NULLIF.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
