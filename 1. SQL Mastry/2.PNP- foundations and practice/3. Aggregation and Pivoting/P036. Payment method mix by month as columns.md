# PNP Q036. Payment method mix by month as columns

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![MEDIUM](<../../_badges/medium.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 36, pp. 61–63. **Difficulty:** Medium.

**Primary topic:** Pivot with FILTER. **Also covers:** DATE_TRUNC grouping, LEFT JOIN, Catch-all column.

**Business scenario.** The payments team sends a monthly sheet to the finance controller showing how customers paid. The controller reads it across, one row per month with one column per method, so a shift from cards and cash on delivery to UPI is visible at a glance. A long format (one row per month and method) is not acceptable for this sheet.

**Task.** For each order month, return the number of orders paid by UPI, Credit Card, Debit Card, Net Banking, Cash on Delivery and Wallet (EMI and Pay Later go to the catch-all column) as separate columns. Add other_or_unknown for orders whose payment type is missing or not in that list, and total_orders . Sort by month, latest first.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
order_month : first day of the month
upi , credit_card , debit_card , net_banking , cash_on_delivery , wallet : order counts
other_or_unknown : orders with no matching payment row or a type outside the list
total_orders : all orders in the month
```

**Tables required:** Orders , Payments.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** turning rows into columns with conditional aggregation, COUNT(*) FILTER , month grouping with DATE_TRUNC, a catch-all column so the pivot reconciles.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
