# PNP Q084. Delivery time by state and shipper with subtotals

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 84, pp. 160–162. **Difficulty:** Hard.

**Primary topic:** GROUP BY ROLLUP with GROUPING(). **Also covers:** Small-cell suppression with CASE, Date validity filters, LEFT JOIN with labelled unknowns, Date arithmetic.

**Business scenario.** The logistics team is deciding which courier to prefer in each state. They want one table: average days from order to delivery for every state and shipper pair, a subtotal for each state across all shippers, and a grand total. Averages from a handful of orders swing wildly and have caused bad decisions before, so any cell with fewer than five delivered orders must show its count but not its average.

**Task.** For delivered orders with a valid date sequence, report the count and average delivery days by state and shipper, with state subtotals and a grand total in the same result. Label subtotal and total rows clearly, and keep them distinct from customers whose state is missing. Show avg_delivery_days only when the row has at least five delivered orders.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
state : customer state, Unknown when missing or unmatched, All states on the grand total row
shipper_name : shipper, Unknown shipper when missing or unmatched, All shippers on subtotal and total rows
row_level : State x shipper , State subtotal or Grand total
delivered_orders : orders counted in the row
avg_delivery_days : average of DeliveryDate - OrderDate , 2 decimals, NULL when delivered_orders < 5
```

**Tables required:** Orders , Customers , Shippers.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** GROUP BY ROLLUP , GROUPING() to tell subtotal rows from NULL data, small-cell suppression with CASE , validity filters on dates, LEFT JOINs with labelled unknowns, sorting subtotals under their detail rows.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
