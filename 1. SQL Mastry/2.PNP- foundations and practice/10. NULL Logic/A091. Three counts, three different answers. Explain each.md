**Task.** Three counts, three different answers. Explain each.

Explain how the three counts in this query differ:

```sql
SELECT
    COUNT(*) AS all_rows,
    COUNT(manager_id) AS non_null_mgr,
    COUNT(DISTINCT manager_id) AS uniq_mgr
FROM employees;
```
