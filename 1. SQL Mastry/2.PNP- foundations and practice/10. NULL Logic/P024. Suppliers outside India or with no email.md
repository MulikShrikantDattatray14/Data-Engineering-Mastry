# PNP Q024. Suppliers outside India or with no email

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 24, pp. 40–42. **Difficulty:** Easy.

**Primary topic:** NOT and OR with NULLs. **Also covers:** IS NULL, CASE issue label.

**Business scenario.** Procurement compliance runs a supplier review each quarter. Suppliers outside India need import paperwork on file, and suppliers with no email cannot receive purchase orders from the new e-procurement system. A supplier with no country recorded is also a problem, because nobody can confirm it is domestic.

**Task.** Return every supplier whose country is not India, whose country is missing, or whose email is missing or blank. Add an issue column naming every problem found. Sort by SupplierID .

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
SupplierID
CompanyName
Country
Email
issue : Outside India , Country missing , No email , or two of these joined by ;
```

**Tables required:** Suppliers.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** OR conditions, three-valued logic with NULL, IS NULL, CASE, CONCAT_WS.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
