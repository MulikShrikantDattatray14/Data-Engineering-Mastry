# PNP Q091. Cumulative value per customer by cohort (LTV curve)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 91, pp. 180–184. **Difficulty:** Expert.

**Primary topic:** Cohort analysis. **Also covers:** Cohort spine with generate_series, Running SUM window, Pivot with FILTER, Maturity masking with an anchored date, Multiple CTEs.

**Business scenario.** The growth team pays for new customers through performance marketing. Finance will only approve a higher acquisition budget if they can see how much order value a typical new customer brings in over their first year, and whether recent cohorts are better or worse than older ones. They want an LTV curve: cumulative order value per acquired customer at fixed points after the first order, one row per acquisition quarter.

**Task.** Group customers into cohorts by the calendar quarter of their first order. For each cohort, report the number of customers and the cumulative order value per customer through month 0, 3, 6 and 12 after each customer's first order month. A value must be NULL, not a partial number, when the cohort has not yet been observed for that long.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
cohort_quarter : first day of the quarter of the customer's first order
cohort_customers : customers whose first order fell in that quarter
value_per_customer_m0 : cumulative Total_Order_Amount in the first order month, divided by cohort_customers
value_per_customer_m3 , value_per_customer_m6 , value_per_customer_m12 : the same, cumulative through month offset 3, 6 and 12
```

**Tables required:** Orders.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** cohort assignment with MIN, calendar month offsets, a dense spine of cohort Ã— month, running SUM with an explicit ROWS frame, pivoting with FILTER, masking immature cells.
