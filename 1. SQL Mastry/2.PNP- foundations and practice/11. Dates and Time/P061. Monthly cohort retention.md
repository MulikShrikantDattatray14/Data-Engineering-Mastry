# PNP Q061. Monthly cohort retention

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![HARD](<../../_badges/hard.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 61, pp. 106–108. **Difficulty:** Hard.

**Primary topic:** Cohort analysis. **Also covers:** Multiple CTEs, Conditional aggregation with FILTER, Anchored partial-period handling.

**Business scenario.** The growth team changed its first-order coupon last year and wants to know whether the customers it brought in actually came back. They group customers by the month of their first order (the cohort) and ask a simple question per cohort: what share of these people placed another order one, two, three and six months later? The table goes into the monthly growth review, so a cohort that has not yet reached month six must show a blank, not a misleading zero.

**Task.** For every cohort month, return the cohort size and the percentage of the cohort who placed at least one order in calendar month 1, 2, 3 and 6 after the cohort month. Month 0 is the cohort month itself. If the data does not fully cover a cohort's month N yet, return NULL for that column. Anchor "how far the data goes" to MAX(OrderDate) .

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
cohort_month : first day of the month of the customer's first order
cohort_size : customers whose first order fell in that month
m1_pct : percentage of the cohort who ordered in the month after the cohort month
m2_pct : same, two months after
m3_pct : same, three months after
m6_pct : same, six months after
```

**Tables required:** Orders.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** customer-month deduplication, MIN per customer for the cohort, month offset arithmetic, conditional aggregation with FILTER , anchoring to the data's last date, NULL for unobserved periods.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
