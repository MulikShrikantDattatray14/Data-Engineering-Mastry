# PNP Q020. Days to ship and days to deliver

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![EASY](<../../_badges/easy.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 20, pp. 35–37. **Difficulty:** Easy.

**Primary topic:** Date arithmetic. **Also covers:** NULL propagation, Casting to date.

**Business scenario.** The logistics team is reviewing November 2025 order by order. For each order they want two numbers: how many days the warehouse took to hand it to the courier, and how many days the customer waited from placing the order to receiving it.

**Task.** For every order placed in November 2025, return the three dates plus days_to_ship (order to ship) and days_to_deliver (order to delivery) in whole days. Missing dates must give NULL, not a made-up number. Sort by OrderDate , then OrderID .

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
OrderID
OrderDate , ShipDate , DeliveryDate
days_to_ship : ShipDate minus OrderDate , in days
days_to_deliver : DeliveryDate minus OrderDate , in days
```

**Tables required:** Orders.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** date subtraction, casting to DATE, NULL in arithmetic, half-open date range.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
