# PNP Q076. Order value from new vs returning customers by month

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![HARD](<../../_badges/hard.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 76, pp. 141–144. **Difficulty:** Hard.

**Primary topic:** Window MIN to tag each customer's first order month. **Also covers:** Conditional aggregation with FILTER, Multiple CTEs, Anchored 12-month window, Contribution percentage.

**Business scenario.** The growth team is splitting next year's budget between acquisition ads and retention campaigns. Before that meeting they want to know how much of each month's order value came from customers who were new that month and how much came from customers who had ordered in an earlier month. A "new customer" in a month is one whose first ever order falls in that calendar month. Every other ordering customer that month is "returning".

**Task.** For each of the last 12 calendar months in the data, return the number of orders and the order value from new customers and from returning customers, and the share of order value that came from returning customers. A customer's first order month must be computed from their full history, not just from the 12 months shown.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
order_month : first day of the month
new_customer_orders : orders placed in the customer's first order month
returning_orders : orders from customers whose first order month is earlier
new_customer_value : SUM(Total_Order_Amount) of new-customer orders
returning_value : SUM(Total_Order_Amount) of returning orders
returning_value_pct : returning value as a percentage of the month's total value
```

**Tables required:** Orders.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** MIN() OVER (PARTITION BY ...) without collapsing rows, conditional aggregation with FILTER , order of evaluation (WHERE before window functions), anchored month range, contribution percentage.
