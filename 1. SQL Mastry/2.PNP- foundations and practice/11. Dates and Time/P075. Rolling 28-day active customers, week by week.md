# PNP Q075. Rolling 28-day active customers, week by week

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 75, pp. 139–141. **Difficulty:** Hard.

**Primary topic:** Rolling distinct counts with a range join. **Also covers:** generate_series week spine, COUNT(DISTINCT) with FILTER, Anchored dates, LEFT JOIN.

**Business scenario.** The growth team reviews two health numbers every Monday. Weekly active customers are distinct customers who placed at least one order in the 7 days ending on the report date. 28-day active customers are distinct customers who ordered at least once in the 28 days ending on that date. They want the last 12 weeks side by side to see whether a dip in one week is noise or the start of a slide.

**Task.** Build 12 weekly report dates, each 7 days apart, with the most recent one on the last order date in the data. For each date, count distinct ordering customers in the trailing 7 days and in the trailing 28 days, both windows ending on and including that date. Weeks with no orders must still appear with 0. Drop any report date whose 28-day window starts before the first order in the data.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
week_end : the report date (last day of the window)
weekly_active_customers : distinct customers with an order in week_end - 6 to week_end
rolling_28d_active_customers : distinct customers with an order in week_end - 27 to week_end
```

**Tables required:** Orders.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** date spine with generate_series , range join on a date window, COUNT(DISTINCT) with FILTER , why COUNT(DISTINCT) cannot be a window function, anchored dates, partial-window handling.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
