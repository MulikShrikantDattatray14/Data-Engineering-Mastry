# PNP Q094. Supplier concentration per category (HHI)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 94, pp. 189–193. **Difficulty:** Expert.

**Primary topic:** Share-squared sums (HHI). **Also covers:** Window SUM for shares, ROW_NUMBER for the top supplier, CASE bands, LEFT JOIN for unknown keys.

**Business scenario.** The supply chain risk team is building the festive-season buying plan. Before demand peaks they want to know which categories lean too hard on one or two suppliers, so they can qualify a backup vendor in time. "Concentration" here means how unevenly a category's list-price value is spread across the suppliers who fulfilled its order lines, measured with the Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI).

**Task.** For every category with sales, work out each supplier's percentage share of the category's list-price value across all order lines. From those shares return the number of suppliers used, the top supplier and its share, the HHI (sum of squared percentage shares, 0 to 10,000) and a concentration band. Sort by HHI, highest first.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
CategoryName : category name, or Unmapped when the product's CategoryID has no Category row
suppliers_used : distinct SupplierID values on the category's lines (an ID with no Suppliers row still counts)
top_supplier : CompanyName of the largest supplier, or Unknown supplier <id>
top_supplier_share_pct : the top supplier's share of category value, 2 dp
hhi : sum of squared percentage shares, rounded to a whole number
concentration_band : Single source , Highly concentrated (above 2,500), Moderately concentrated (1,500 to 2,500) or Unconcentrated
(below 1,500)
```

**Tables required:** OrderDetails , Products , Category , Suppliers.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** line-grain aggregation, window SUM for shares, sums of squares, ROW_NUMBER with a tie-break, CASE banding, labelling unmatched keys.
