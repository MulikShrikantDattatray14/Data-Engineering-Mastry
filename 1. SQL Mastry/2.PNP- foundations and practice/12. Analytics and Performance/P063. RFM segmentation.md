## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 63, pp. 111–113. **Difficulty:** Hard.

**Primary topic:** Customer segmentation. **Also covers:** NTILE scoring, Multiple CTEs, CASE segment rules, Anchored recency.

**Business scenario.** The CRM team runs a different email track for each customer segment: early access for the best customers, a nudge for those slipping away, and a win-back coupon for the dormant ones. They want every customer scored 1 to 5 on recency (how recently they ordered), frequency (how many orders) and monetary value (how much they spent), then placed in one named segment by fixed rules. Only registered customers can be emailed.

**Task.** For each registered customer who has ordered, compute recency in days since their last order, measured from the latest order date in the data, the number of orders, and the total order value. Score each metric into quintiles (5 = best). Customers with identical values must get identical scores.
