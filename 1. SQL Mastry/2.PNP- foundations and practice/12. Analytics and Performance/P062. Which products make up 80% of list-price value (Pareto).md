# PNP Q062. Which products make up 80% of list-price value (Pareto)

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![HARD](<../../_badges/hard.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 62, pp. 109–111. **Difficulty:** Hard.

**Primary topic:** Pareto analysis. **Also covers:** Running total window with an explicit ROWS frame, Contribution percentage, CTE aggregation.

**Business scenario.** The merchandising team is deciding which products get guaranteed shelf space and stock buffers in the new warehouse. Their rule: protect the smallest set of products that together account for 80% of sales value, and review the rest as the long tail. Sales value here means list-price value, since order lines carry no price of their own.

**Task.** Rank products by list-price value ( Quantity * Sale_Price , summed over all order lines), highest first. For each product show its share of the total and the running share. Label a product Top 80% if it is needed to reach 80% of the total, including the product that crosses the line, and Long tail otherwise.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
ProductID , Product
list_price_value : SUM of Quantity * Sale_Price for the product
pct_of_total : the product's share of all list-price value, 2 dp
cumulative_pct : running share from the top product down to this one, 2 dp
pareto_group : Top 80% or Long tail
```

**Tables required:** OrderDetails , Products.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** aggregation in a CTE, SUM() OVER () for a grand total, running SUM with an explicit ROWS frame, deterministic ordering for ties, contribution percentages, CASE on a running value.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
