# PNP Q098. Category association rules: support, confidence, lift

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![UNRATED](<../../_badges/unrated.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 98, pp. 203–206. **Difficulty:** Expert.

**Primary topic:** Market basket analysis. **Also covers:** Distinct order-category baskets, Self join on OrderID, Multiple CTEs, Ratio metrics with a minimum-support filter.

**Business scenario.** The merchandising team is redesigning the cart page. When a shopper adds something from one category, a strip below suggests a second category. The team wants the suggestion to be a category that the first one genuinely pulls along, not just whichever category is most popular overall. They have asked for association rules between categories, with enough volume behind each rule to trust it.

**Task.** Treat each order as a basket of the distinct categories in it. For every ordered pair of categories (A, B) bought together in at least 2% of baskets, return the number of baskets containing both, the support, the confidence of the rule "A leads to B" and the lift. Sort by lift, highest first.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
category_a : the category already in the basket (the rule's left side)
category_b : the category being suggested (the right side)
orders_with_both : baskets containing both A and B
support_pct : orders_with_both as a percentage of all baskets, 2 dp
confidence_a_to_b_pct : orders_with_both as a percentage of baskets containing A, 2 dp
lift : confidence of A to B divided by the share of baskets that contain B, 3 dp
```

**Tables required:** OrderDetails , Products , Category.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** deduplicating to a set per order, self join on a shared key, pair counting, ratios built from three different denominators, thresholds as parameters.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
