# PNP Q067. Median vs mean order value by payment type

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![HARD](<../../_badges/hard.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 67, pp. 121–123. **Difficulty:** Hard.

**Primary topic:** Median and percentiles. **Also covers:** PERCENTILE_CONT WITHIN GROUP, LEFT JOIN with Unknown label, Skew ratio.

**Business scenario.** The payments team is negotiating gateway fees, and some fee slabs depend on "typical order size". The average order value per payment method is easy to pull, but a handful of very large orders can drag an average far above what a normal order looks like. They want the mean and the median side by side for every payment type, plus a ratio that shows how skewed each method is.

**Task.** For each payment type, return the number of orders with a known order total, the mean order value, the median order value and the ratio of mean to median. Orders whose PaymentID is NULL or does not exist in Payments go under Unknown . Sort by the ratio, most skewed first.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
payment_type : PaymentType , or Unknown
orders : orders with a non-NULL Total_Order_Amount
mean_order_value : AVG(Total_Order_Amount) , 2 dp
median_order_value : 50th percentile of Total_Order_Amount , 2 dp
mean_to_median_ratio : mean divided by median, 2 dp
```

**Tables required:** Orders , Payments.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** PERCENTILE_CONT(0.5) WITHIN GROUP (ORDER BY ...) , type of its result, NULL handling inside aggregates, LEFT JOIN plus COALESCE label, NULLIF in a ratio, a CTE to avoid repeating the median expression.
