# PNP Q037. Prove no order was paid with a method that is not allowed

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![MEDIUM](<../../_badges/medium.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 37, pp. 63–65. **Difficulty:** Medium.

**Primary topic:** LEFT JOIN with a normalised flag check. **Also covers:** Conditional status with CASE, Pre-aggregation, Business-rule validation.

**Business scenario.** The risk team has switched off some payment methods, marked Allowed = 'No' in Payments . Before the quarterly audit they want proof that no order was taken through a disallowed method. "Zero rows came back" is not proof an auditor accepts, because a wrong join also returns zero rows. They want one row per payment method with its flag, its order count and a status, so the clean result is visible.

**Task.** For every row in Payments , return the raw Allowed value, a normalised yes/no, the number of orders and their total value, and a status: OK (allowed and used), Allowed, unused , Not allowed, unused , or VIOLATION: not allowed but used . A blank or NULL flag counts as not allowed. Sort violations first, then by PaymentID .

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
PaymentID , PaymentType
allowed_raw : the value as stored
is_allowed : TRUE or FALSE after normalising
orders : orders paid with this method (0 if none)
order_value : sum of Total_Order_Amount (0 if none)
status : one of the four labels above
```

**Tables required:** Payments , Orders.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** driving the result from the lookup table so unused methods appear, aggregating orders before joining, normalising a text flag, turning a rule into an explicit status.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.

### Interview follow-up (Step 9)

The risk team adds a DisabledFrom date to Payments. How would you change the query so an order only counts as a violation when it was placed after its method was disabled?

### Sample input and expected output

| PaymentID | PaymentType | Allowed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | UPI | Y |
| 2 | Card | N |
| 3 | Cash | NULL |

**Input — `Orders`**

| OrderID | PaymentID | Total_Order_Amount |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | 1 | 100 |
| 11 | 2 | 50 |

**Expected output**

| PaymentID | PaymentType | allowed_raw | is_allowed | orders | order_value | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | Card | N | FALSE | 1 | 50 | VIOLATION: not allowed but used |
| 1 | UPI | Y | TRUE | 1 | 100 | OK |
| 3 | Cash | NULL | FALSE | 0 | 0 | Not allowed, unused |

The disallowed payment with an order is listed before the others.
