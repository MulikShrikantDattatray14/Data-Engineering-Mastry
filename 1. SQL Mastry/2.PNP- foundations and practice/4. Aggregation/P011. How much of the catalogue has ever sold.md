# PNP Q011. How much of the catalogue has ever sold?

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![EASY](<../../_badges/easy.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 11, pp. 24–25. **Difficulty:** Easy.

**Business scenario.** The merchandising head suspects the catalogue is far bigger than what customers actually buy. Before a clean-up, she wants four numbers in one row: how many products are listed, how many sale lines exist, how many distinct products have sold at least once, and what share of the catalogue that is.

**Task.** Starting from every product, attach its order lines. In one row, return the rows produced by that join, the number of order lines, the number of listed products, the number of products that sold at least once, and the percentage of listed products that sold.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
rows_after_join : rows produced by Products LEFT JOIN OrderDetails
sale_lines : order lines matched to a product
listed_products : distinct products in the catalogue
products_sold : distinct products with at least one line
pct_sold : products_sold as a percentage of listed_products , 2 decimals
```

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.

### Sample input and expected output

*Illustrative example; the source gives output columns but no sample rows. Only the columns needed for this example are shown.*

**Input — `Products`**

| ProductID | Product |
|---|---|
| 1 | Pen |
| 2 | Bag |
| 3 | Cup |

**Input — `OrderDetails`**

| OrderDetailID | OrderID | ProductID |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10 | 1 |
| 2 | 11 | 1 |
| 3 | 11 | 2 |

**Expected output**

| rows_after_join | sale_lines | listed_products | products_sold | pct_sold |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 3 | 3 | 2 | 66.67 |

The unsold Cup still contributes one row to the left join, giving four joined rows.
