# PNP Q041. Most expensive product in each category

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![MEDIUM](<../../_badges/medium.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 41, pp. 70–72. **Difficulty:** Medium.

**Primary topic:** ROW_NUMBER vs RANK for top-1 per group. **Also covers:** Ties, DISTINCT ON, LEFT JOIN for unmapped categories.

**Business scenario.** The merchandising team is planning a premium banner on each category page. The banner shows the category's most expensive product by current Sale_Price . If two products tie for the top price, both go on the banner, because picking one at random would favour a brand without a reason.

**Task.** For each category, return the product or products with the highest Sale_Price . Keep every product that ties for first. Products whose CategoryID has no Category row are grouped under Unmapped . Products with no price cannot be the most expensive. Sort by category name, then ProductID .

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
CategoryName : the category's name, or Unmapped
ProductID , Product
Sale_Price
```

**Tables required:** Products , Category.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** window functions with PARTITION BY, the difference between ROW_NUMBER and RANK on ties, NULL ordering in DESC sorts, PostgreSQL's DISTINCT ON.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.

### Interview follow-up (Step 9)

The banner now has room for the top three prices per category, and two products tied at second place should both appear without pushing the third price out. Which ranking function would you switch to?

|---|---|

**Input — `Products`**

| ProductID | Product | CategoryID | Sale_Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tea | 1 | 10 |
| 2 | Coffee | 1 | 10 |
| 3 | Unknown drink | 1 | NULL |
| 4 | Cookie | 2 | 5 |

**Expected output**

| CategoryName | ProductID | Product | Sale_Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Drinks | 1 | Tea | 10 |
| Drinks | 2 | Coffee | 10 |
| Snacks | 4 | Cookie | 5 |

Both Drinks products priced at 10 tie for first.
