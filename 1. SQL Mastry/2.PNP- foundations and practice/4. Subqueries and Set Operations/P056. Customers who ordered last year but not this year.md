# PNP Q056. Customers who ordered last year but not this year

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 56, pp. 97–98. **Difficulty:** Medium.

**Primary topic:** EXCEPT set operation. **Also covers:** Anchored year boundaries, NOT EXISTS alternative, Join back for details.

**Business scenario.** The retention team is planning a win-back campaign. The target list is customers who were active in the previous calendar year and have placed nothing in the latest calendar year. "Latest year" is the calendar year of the most recent order in the data, so the list works on any extract. For prioritising calls, the team wants each customer's last order date and how many orders they placed in the previous year.

**Task.** Find customers with at least one order in the previous calendar year and no order in the latest calendar year. For each, return the customer name, their last order date and the number of orders they placed in the previous year. Orders whose CustomerID has no row in Customers still count; label the name Unknown customer . Sort by last order date, most recent first, then customer ID.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
CustomerID
customer_name : first and last name, or Unknown customer
last_order_date : the customer's most recent order date
orders_prev_year : orders placed in the previous calendar year
```

**Tables required:** Orders , Customers.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** EXCEPT for set difference, set operations on a single key, anchored half-open year ranges, joining back to fetch details.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
