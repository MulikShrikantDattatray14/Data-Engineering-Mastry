# PNP Q028. Payment mix: share of orders and value

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![MEDIUM](<../../_badges/medium.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 28, pp. 47–49. **Difficulty:** Medium.

**Primary topic:** Percentage of total with a scalar subquery. **Also covers:** LEFT JOIN, COALESCE labels, GROUP BY.

**Business scenario.** The payments team is renegotiating gateway fees, and fees are charged per transaction for some methods and as a share of value for others. They need each payment type's share of order count and its share of order value in one table. A method can carry many small orders or a few large ones, so the two shares can tell different stories.

**Task.** For each payment type, return the number of orders, its percentage of all orders, the total order value and its percentage of all order value. Orders whose PaymentID is NULL or matches no payment row go under Unknown . Use scalar subqueries for the two denominators. Sort by orders, highest first.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
payment_type : PaymentType , or Unknown
orders : number of orders
pct_of_orders : orders as a percentage of all orders (2 dp)
order_value : sum of Total_Order_Amount
pct_of_value : order value as a percentage of all order value (2 dp)
```

**Tables required:** Orders , Payments.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** scalar subquery as a denominator, percentage of total, LEFT JOIN with a fallback label, integer versus numeric division.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
