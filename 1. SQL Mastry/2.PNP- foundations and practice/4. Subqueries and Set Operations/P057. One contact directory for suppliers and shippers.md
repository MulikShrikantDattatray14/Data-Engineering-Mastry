# PNP Q057. One contact directory for suppliers and shippers

![NOT SOLVED YET](<../../_badges/not-solved-yet.svg>) ![MEDIUM](<../../_badges/medium.svg>)

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 57, pp. 99–100. **Difficulty:** Medium.

**Primary topic:** UNION ALL vs UNION. **Also covers:** Literal type column, Column alignment and NULL placeholders.

**Business scenario.** The partner operations desk handles calls from suppliers and courier companies, and today they switch between two screens to find a phone number. They want one directory with every partner, a column saying which kind of partner it is, and the contact fields both tables can supply. Shippers have no email or country on file, and the directory should show that as blank rather than inventing values.

**Task.** Build one list of all suppliers and all shippers with the columns below. A company that is both a supplier and a shipper must appear twice, once per role. Sort by company name, then partner type, then ID.

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
partner_type : Supplier or Shipper
partner_id : SupplierID or ShipperID
company_name
phone
email : NULL for shippers
country : NULL for shippers
```

**Tables required:** Suppliers , Shippers.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** stacking results with UNION ALL, how UNION differs, matching columns by position, literal columns, typed NULL placeholders, ORDER BY on a combined result.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.

### Interview follow-up (Step 9)

The desk wants to find phone numbers shared by more than one row of this directory, across both roles. How would you write that?

|---|---|---|---|---|

**Input — `Shippers`**

| ShipperID | CompanyName | Phone |

| 2 | Alpha | 222 |

**Expected output**

| partner_type | partner_id | company_name | phone | email | country |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shipper | 2 | Alpha | 222 | NULL | NULL |
| Supplier | 1 | Alpha | 111 | alpha@example.com | India |

Alpha appears twice because the supplier and shipper are separate roles.
