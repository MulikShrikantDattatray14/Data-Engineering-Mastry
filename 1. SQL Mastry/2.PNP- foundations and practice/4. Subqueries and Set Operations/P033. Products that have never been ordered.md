# PNP Q033. Products that have never been ordered

## Question and image

**Source:** [100 SQL Interview Questions](<../Source Materials/Updated_100_SQL_Interview_Questions.pdf>), question 33, pp. 56–58. **Difficulty:** Medium.

**Primary topic:** NOT EXISTS anti-join. **Also covers:** NOT IN NULL trap, LEFT JOIN anti-join alternative.

**Business scenario.** The merchandising team pays for shelf space in the warehouse and wants to delist products that have never sold. Before anything is removed from the catalogue, they want the list of products with zero order lines in the entire history. A product that appeared in even one order line, in any quantity, is not on the list.

**Task.** Return every product in Products that does not appear in any row of OrderDetails . Sort by CategoryID , then ProductID .

**Expected output (as supplied):**

```text
ProductID , Product , Brand , CategoryID , Sale_Price
```

**Tables required:** Products , OrderDetails.

**SQL concepts named in the source:** NOT EXISTS, correlated subquery, why NOT IN fails with a NULL in the list, LEFT JOIN with IS NULL on the join key.

The source is a text-based PDF; no separate question image was supplied.
