# 10. SQL Big Countries

## Question and image

A country is **big** when its `area` is at least **3000000** square kilometres **or** its `population` is at least **25000000**. Return the `name`, `population`, and `area` of every big country. No output order is specified.

**Example 1**

Input — `World`:

| name | continent | area | population | gdp |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Afghanistan | Asia | 652230 | 25500100 | 20343000000 |
| Albania | Europe | 28748 | 2831741 | 12960000000 |
| Algeria | Africa | 2381741 | 37100000 | 188681000000 |
| Andorra | Europe | 468 | 78115 | 3712000000 |
| Angola | Africa | 1246700 | 20609294 | 100990000000 |

Expected output:

| name | population | area |
|---|---:|---:|
| Afghanistan | 25500100 | 652230 |
| Algeria | 37100000 | 2381741 |

Afghanistan and Algeria reach the population threshold. None of the sample countries reaches the area threshold.
